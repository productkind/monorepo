## Your Lovable story
### What have you created with Lovable? Share a viewable link plus context about the project.

The very first product I built with Lovable was a User Story Coach, back in February 2025 (https://story-partner.lovable.app). It's no longer operational, the database behind it is paused.

Since then, I built many things, the most complex product that I turned into a business is Little Parrot (https://littleparrot.app/). It's a micro-learning platform where we offer women hands-on courses to build with AI. During International Women's Day, 663 people started our "Build your first app with Lovable" course. It helped a huge number of women get over the confidence gap and start building with Lovable, and I have the LinkedIn posts to prove it. Just some teasers (not to use up all the characters):
