## Your Lovable story
### What have you created with Lovable? Share a viewable link plus context about the project.
0/1000

My first Lovable build was a User Story Coach, back in February 2025 (story-partner.lovable.app). It's paused now, but it's why I kept building.

The one I turned into a business is Little Parrot (littleparrot.app), a micro-learning platform where women learn to build with AI. It's built with Lovable, and it teaches people to build with Lovable. So I run into the same problems my learners do, usually a bit earlier.

Before International Women's Day we gave our "Build your first app with Lovable" course free for two weeks, so more women could make the most of Lovable's free day. 663 people started it. What I didn't expect was how many of them wrote about it publicly, saying it got them over the confidence gap and into Lovable for the first time:
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/elena-martin-hernandez-h_a-week-ago-my-daughter-started-nursery-and-share-7437458915229044737-jBwc/

So I've watched Lovable change quite a bit over the past year and a half, and Little Parrot grew up with it.

### Why do you want to represent Lovable in your community? What would make this a fantastic experience for you?
0/1000

I've been part of the Lovable community from the start. First, going through Lovable Shipped, and ending at #40. Then SheBuilds S01 as a participant, then SheBuilds S02 as a volunteer, supporting builders on Discord, and also offering them two of our courses for free to help them build their product with Lovable. Then at an in-person event in Singapore at IWD where the Lovable ambassador used our course content word-for-word without us knowing her, because she found it more useful than the material she had access to. We also gave our "Build your first app with Lovable" course for free for two weeks before IWD to encourage more women to take advantage of the free Lovable day.

And throughout all this, I shared and promoted Lovable, because it democratises software development. I’ve seen how it can empower women to start their businesses or build that extra income stream.
