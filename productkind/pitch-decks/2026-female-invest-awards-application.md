# Female Invest Women in Action Awards 2026: Little Parrot application

Applicant: Kinga Magyar

Deadline: 23:59 BST, 18 October 2026 (https://womeninaction.femaleinvest.com/)

---

## Which award are you applying for?

The Builder

## What's your idea/business called?

Little Parrot

## In one sentence, what is it?

Little Parrot teaches women to build their own products, and their own income, with AI, and profits above a cap buy them time to build.

## What problem are you solving, and why does it matter?

Women have fewer ways to build an income of their own. Investors rarely back them: in 2025, all-female founding teams got 1.3% of European venture capital (PitchBook). And everything else demanding their time leaves little for their own ideas. When our learners stop a course, the No. 1 reason they give is time.

AI opens a new route: a woman with an idea can now build the product herself. But it still takes technical knowledge. "I don't know what I don't know," a woman told me at a women-only meetup. In about 230 quotes we gathered from women building with AI, they got stuck in the same places: saving versions of their work, storing data and launching. So their ideas stay prototypes and never reach a paying customer.

That's what we're solving: women need the technical knowledge to launch a product, and the time to build it.

One in six of our 933 learners has finished a course, where online courses typically see fewer than one in ten. One built an employee feedback tool for her clients, another a tool that pulls the costs out of investment documents, and another an app for families caring for someone with dementia.

## What stage are you at?

Launched and generating revenue

## What's your vision? Don't hold back, we love big goals.

By 2030, Little Parrot will be an incubator that teaches women to build their own products and buys 100 of them a year the time to build.

AI means a woman with an idea can build it herself, without investors or developers, and earn her own income. Women were left behind once before: in 1984, 37% of US computer science graduates were women, and by 2010, only 18%. A Harvard Business School paper finds women use AI tools at a rate about 16% lower than men, and the gap is widest on the newest AI coding tools. We don't want them to miss this chance too.

We're two founders who built Little Parrot with AI, so our costs stay low. We cap our profits, and everything above it funds women.

Women who can afford it pay to learn. Companies need the same skills for their product managers and can pay far more than individuals, so in 2027 we're focusing on them. Two have already put our learning journey forward for next year's budget. Bigger sales mean more profit above the cap, so the more we sell, the more women we fund.

Every woman we fund will build a product of her own, earn an income of her own, and show the women around her they can build too.

## If you win, what will this money help you do?

£10,000 starts our incubator with five women and finds the company customers whose sales will fund the next five.

Each woman gets £1,000 to spend on whatever gives her time to build: a few hours of childcare, meals she doesn't have to cook or help at home. She decides, no two women have the same responsibilities. She also gets free course access and a monthly call with us. We'll open applications to our community and ask nonprofits like the Cherie Blair Foundation for Women and ReDI School to share the call. We'll pick the five with the clearest problem, user and way to earn.

£5,000 goes on reaching customers, our No. 1 barrier to growth. Two channels already bring us learners: live events, and free courses at women's buildathons, where sign-ups grew nearly eightfold in three months.

- £3,000 on live events for product managers, whose companies pay for their learning.
- £2,000 on online promotion: LinkedIn ads, cross-promotion with women's communities and referral rewards.

We'll move money to the channel that brings paying customers most cheaply.

By the end of 2027, we want all five women's products live with their own customers, and the next five women funded by our sales.
