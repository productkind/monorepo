## Which award are you applying for?

The Builder

## What's your idea/business called?

Little Parrot

## In one sentence, what is it?

Little Parrot helps women with a business idea build a digital product themselves with AI, in hands-on courses that fit around a busy life.

## What problem are you solving, and why does it matter?

Women with a business idea can now build a product themselves with AI app builders like Lovable. But these tools are expensive, and not every woman can afford a paid plan.

At Little Parrot, women build their own product with AI as they learn, in short courses they can do after the children are asleep. Course by course, she plans her app, builds a working version and publishes it. 933 learners have signed up and 160 have finished at least one course. Our learners' No. 1 complaint is running out of credits on the free plan halfway through a course. A learner who runs out has to wait or pay before she knows whether her idea works. Our Open Wings Scholarship already gives lifetime access to our courses, but we can't also pay for the AI tools women need to build.

Women-led businesses are underfunded, so a woman who can build the product herself, without raising money first, has a route to economic independence. We've both seen how access to technology can change a woman's career. I started mine in luxury fashion, have spent the past decade in tech and have been mentoring women. My co-founder has mentored women and girls in software development for 11 years.

## What stage are you at?

Launched and generating revenue

## What's your vision? Don't hold back, we love big goals.

Our mission is to close the gender gap in AI adoption.

A gap like this has opened before, in computing. In 1984, the share of women studying computer science in the US stopped rising and then fell. NPR's Planet Money traced part of it to the first home computers, which were marketed mainly to boys. AI app builders now let anyone with an idea build software, and we don't want history to repeat itself.

Building software gives you power, and we want more women to have it. When a woman builds her own product, she earns her own income, solves problems her community has, and shows other women they can build too.

So we want Little Parrot to be where women learn to build with AI: short, practical courses in a kind space, where a woman can ask the questions she thinks are too basic.

We're building a profitable, purpose-driven business, and our Open Wings Scholarship already gives lifetime access to underrepresented groups in tech. This award would start a credit fund we run every year, and we'll ask the AI companies our learners build with to co-fund it. Our big goal is to grow it into an incubator that funds women's ideas.
