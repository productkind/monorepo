## Final ranking

Scored on: individual buyer intent + long-tail depth + commercial demand + achievable competition for a small website.

| # | Cluster | Relevant vol (US+UK) | Commercial vol (US) | Median CPC | Median KD | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Client operations (consultants/freelancers) | ~24,500 | ~16,300 | $14.60 | 25 | Best overall. Buyers are visibly individuals, SERPs already have Reddit #1 and solo bloggers ranking, and KD 14–16 entry points carry $19–31 CPCs. |
| 2 | Client reporting automation | ~16,300 | ~13,500 | $13.80 | 25 | Nearly 100% commercial intent, two KD-7 gems, and a proven "alternative to expensive SaaS" angle (`agencyanalytics alternative`, KD 13). Small-agency buyers are individuals. |
| 3 | SaaS SEO for founders | ~25,200 | ~12,000 | $12.60 | 22 | Biggest pool and lowest KD, but split in two: high-CPC half is companies hiring agencies; the individual half (`diy seo`, `saas seo course`) is smaller and lower-CPC. Great fit if the product teaches founders to DIY. |
| 4 | Customer feedback synthesis for PMs | ~16,900 | ~8,900 | $21.60 | 26 | Highest CPCs, but the money terms are enterprise VoC (Qualtrics/Medallia wall). The individual-PM wedge ("analyse interviews/feedback with AI") is real but tiny today — an emerging bet, not a current demand pool. |
| 5 | Validating a vibe-coded app | ~12,900 | ~5,700 | $6.40 | 28 | Deepest bench of zero-competition long-tails and perfect audience match, but intent is informational and CPCs are the lowest — people researching, not buying. Best used as top-of-funnel content feeding a paid product, not as the product's core keyword set. |

### Key cross-cluster insights:

- Clusters 1 and 2 are the same buyer (solo consultant / freelancer / small agency) at two different workflow steps. Combined they're ~24k US commercial volume with median KD ~25 — the strongest case for a single digital product (e.g. "client operations system for solo consultants").
- Enterprise contamination is worst in Cluster 3 and moderate in Cluster 5 — in both, the highest-CPC keywords are exactly the wrong buyer. Don't be seduced by the $30+ CPCs there.
- Cluster 4 has the best audience–product fit but the weakest search economics — its zero-competition long-tails are ideal supporting content, not a headline opportunity.
- Fastest wins anywhere in this dataset: `seo client reporting` (KD 7, 1,000/mo US), `client dashboard software` (KD 7, $18.46 CPC), `technical seo for saas` (KD 7, 480/mo), `b2b saas seo` (KD 11, $32.13 CPC), `app launch checklist` (KD 8).

One caveat: built-in Semrush tools are organic-only — CPC and density reflect keyword-level data; advertiser identity (who's actually bidding) would need the Semrush connector on your own subscription if you ever want that built into the app.
