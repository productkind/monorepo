# Outcome- and problem-led Semrush seed keywords

**Audience:** non-technical software Product Managers  
**Prepared:** 27 August 2026  
**Purpose:** discover related and long-tail searches using the language Product Managers use to describe their work problems and desired outcomes.

## How this list differs from the first seed set

The earlier Semrush research started with technical concepts such as APIs, deployment, logs and technical debt. This list starts with the result the Product Manager wants: confidence, credibility, judgement, independence and speed.

The phrases below are search-shaped hypotheses grounded in the first-person language recorded in the needs research. They have not yet been checked for volume, difficulty or intent. Zero-volume seeds may still reveal useful related queries in Semrush.

## Suggested Semrush workflow

1. Run Priority 1 seeds by outcome cluster rather than as one undifferentiated batch.
2. Keep phrase match and related-keyword results separate. Phrase match tests the wording; related results reveal the vocabulary searchers actually use.
3. Export search volume, keyword difficulty, intent, cost per click, trend and the ranking URLs for the US and UK databases.
4. Label audience fit manually. Many results for confidence, scope, SQL, prototyping and software delivery will serve engineers, founders, project managers or job seekers.
5. Preserve zero-volume phrases. They can still work as social hooks and landing-page language even when they are poor organic-search targets.
