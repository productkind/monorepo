The other ~94 seeds returned no row at all — they are conversational phrasings below Semrush's reporting threshold, not zero-volume keywords. That is a real finding, not a failure: this topic space has **very little head-term search demand and a lot of long-tail conversational demand**. The strategy below is therefore built from Semrush's *related* and *question* keyword discovery around the head terms your seeds imply.

**Endpoint limitations, stated plainly:**
- The built-in Semrush tools **do not return search intent**. Intent labels below are classified from phrase structure and SERP composition, and marked *(inferred)*.
- The built-in SERP tool returns ranking domains and URLs plus difficulty, **not page titles or SERP-feature flags**. Presence of "Video", "Short video", or "People Also Ask" **could not be read from the endpoint**. Social selection therefore uses question-keyword status + SERP composition (YouTube/Reddit/Medium presence) as the proxy, and this substitution is flagged per cluster.
- The tools are organic-only. Full SERP-feature data and Organic Research endpoints require the Semrush connector.

**Traffic-flow logic:**
- LP-7 and LP-1 carry the two highest-volume social terms (`what is vibe coding` 49,500/mo, `what is a root cause analysis` 90,500/mo). These are your top-of-funnel engines — the social content is where the volume lives, the landing page is where the intent converts.
- LP-2 (certification/course) has almost no informational social surface of its own. Feed it from LP-1's LinkedIn content with a course CTA rather than trying to make "certification" a social topic.
- `mvp vs prototype` is the one term where a **YouTube video already ranks organically (#6)** — publishing video there earns both social reach and a Google SERP slot.

## Recommended build order

1. **LP-7 `ai prototyping`** (KD 24, Lenny's Newsletter at #1 proves content wins, perfectly on-brand) — plus the biggest social volume behind it.
2. **LP-6 `product analytics`** — UK `product analytics tools` at KD 25 / $19.18 CPC is the best commercial risk/reward in the report.
3. **LP-1 `technical product manager`** — KD 29 US / 17 UK, Reddit at #1 means practitioner voice ranks.
4. **LP-4 `technical debt` + build vs buy** — a Medium post by one PM already ranks #3 for build-vs-buy.
5. **LP-3 `technical feasibility`** — weak glossary competition, and the estimation secondaries carry $15–23 CPCs.
6. **LP-5 `bug triage`** — soft SERP but small volume; scope tightly to avoid gaming traffic.
7. **LP-2 `product management course`** — build UK-first (KD 33 vs 66 US); highest CPCs but hardest organic.
