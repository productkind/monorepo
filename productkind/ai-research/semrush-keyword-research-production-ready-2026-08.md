# Semrush search: building safe, reliable and maintainable app

**Targeted niche:** Experienced women with deep domain expertise who have built, or are actively buildin, a customer-facing app with AI, have no technical cofounder, and need to make it safe, reliable and maintainable before real customers, data or payments are involved.


**Date:** 2026-08-22

All research is complete — 242 keywords × 6 databases, plus related/question keywords and SERP pulls for the 18-keyword shortlist. Full results below in copyable markdown.

Legend: Volumes = searches/month per country database. KD, CPC and Competitive Density are from the US database. `—` = no data in Semrush. Fit: A = exact target audience, B = adjacent (developers/agencies/generic no-code), C = irrelevant. Stage: 1 Choosing a tool, 2 Building, 3 Debugging, 4 Preparing to launch, 5 Security and data, 6 Payments and auth, 7 Testing, 8 Maintenance/ownership, 9 Course purchase.
