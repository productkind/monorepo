## What this pull shows

1. `lovable vibe coding` is a winnable target: about 950 searches a month across the four databases, KD 28, and a results page led by personal blog posts, with two courses (ONLC at #3, LinkedIn Learning at #5) and Lovable's own site only at #4. A post by a non-developer ranks at #6.
2. The KD 0 on `lovable course`, `learn lovable`, `lovable training` and `lovable tutorial for beginners` was missing data: Semrush has no results page for any of them. The volumes (about 20 a month each) are too small for it to track.
3. People do say "app idea" (480 a month in both the US and UK), but the questions around it are mostly about patenting, protecting, selling or pitching the idea. In the US top 20, the patent, protect and copyright questions add up to about 1,080 a month and the selling questions to about 500. Many people with an app idea assume someone else will have to build it.
4. The UK questions include two that match what the learning path teaches: `how do i know if my app idea is viable` (210) and `how to validate an app idea before building` (170). "Must have ideas" is a TV show app and is noise.
5. `i have an app idea` is small (50 US) and its results page is held by forums and development agencies selling builds.
