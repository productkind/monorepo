## 7. Where to focus

1. **Checker/validator cluster** (§3) — thousands of searches a month, moderate competition, $4–11 CPC, and it matches a tool rather than an article.
2. **AI search long tail** (§2) — llm seo, how to rank in chatgpt, structured data for ai: paid interest with low competition, the rare combination.
3. **Plain-language entry pages** (§5) — "how to get my website to show up on google" is the phrase the intended reader actually types.
4. **Tool-specific pages** (§4) — lovable seo and claude code seo are small, winnable, and the visitors are precisely the right people.
5. **FAQ** (§6) — the question lists above, in the searcher's own wording, as published FAQPage entries.

Skip head-on attacks on "schema markup" (97 difficulty) and "generative engine optimization" (77) until the smaller pages are earning.

Semrush covers Google organic results only — it can't see direct visits, social, ads, or email, and traffic figures are estimates rather than counts.
