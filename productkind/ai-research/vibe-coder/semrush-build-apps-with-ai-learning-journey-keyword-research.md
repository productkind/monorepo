### Verdict on "Building Apps with AI"

- It is searched, but only a little: US 110, UK 10, CA 10, AU 10. KD 53, CPC $12.50.
- The results page is for **choosing a tool**: base44, anything.com, Zite, Firebase, Databricks and Zapier's "best AI app builder". A course page is unlikely to rank there.
- The closest searched phrases: "build an app with ai" (US 480, KD 59), "create an app with ai" (590, KD 68) and "how to build an app with ai" (320, KD 60). All are over KD 50 and full of tool vendors.
- **Recommendation:** drop it as the title. Use "build an app with AI" as a phrase in the subtitle and meta description, where its high CPC shows commercial value.

### Should the title include "Lovable"?

| Phrasing | Best learning term | US vol | KD | Note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lovable | how to use lovable / lovable vibe coding | 170 / 720 | 48 / 28 | Lovable's own docs and videos hold the top spots |
| Vibe coding | how to vibe code | 1,600 | 48 | Largest learning demand; course platforms rank |
| Generic AI | build an app with ai | 480 | 59 | Tool-shopping results; KD too high |
| No-code | how to build an app without coding | 210 | 46 | Mixed results; high CPC |

**Verdict:** keep "Lovable" out of the title and put it in the subtitle. Lovable demand is real ("lovable vibe coding" 720, KD 28), but Lovable itself outranks everyone on tutorial searches. Tying the name to one tool also risks the path aging badly if you add other tools or Lovable changes. Lovable belongs in the course titles and the FAQ.
