### Lovable app versus Lovable production

| lovable production | 15 | 0.1 | 0.0 | 0.2 |

`Lovable app` has grown strongly, while the proposed umbrella wording `Lovable production` is almost absent. Related searches for `Lovable app` included app-builder, pricing, GitHub, Replit and Base44 terms.

This means Little Parrot should not rely on “Lovable production” as the phrase people already use. The product can still solve the production-readiness job, but acquisition content must use the concrete tasks and questions people search for.

## Interpretation

1. Claude Code and Lovable are growing quickly in the UK. This strengthens the timing case for Little Parrot's existing AI-building expertise.
2. The chart does **not** prove that Claude Code has a larger monetisable education market. Trends measures relative search attention, and broad brand searches contain documentation, pricing, news and navigational intent.
3. The three non-brand categories are suppressed by the two fast-growing brands. They need separate comparisons before their direction can be judged.
4. Lovable's related queries connect it to GitHub and Supabase, supporting the post-prototype and control/ownership problems found in learner feedback.
5. Claude Code related interest is dominated by setup, price, GitHub and general learning. That supports demand, but the supply audit showed that beginner Claude Code education is already crowded.
6. Lead-magnet interest is persistent and practical, but the related terms point to generic ideas, examples and templates rather than interactive consultant assets.
7. Candidate-specific results favour task and workflow language: `Lovable GitHub` over `Lovable production`, and `AI user research` over `AI for product managers`.

## Effect on the niche decision

The results strengthen the **timing** score for Little Parrot's AI-building territory and make the user-research workflow more promising, but they do not pass the absolute-volume gate or change the provisional ranking:

- Lovable prototype to production remains the first validation test because it combines rising tool interest with the strongest first-party problem signal.
- Lovable to Claude Code has the strongest growth context, but the existing supply is too extensive for a broad beginner product.
- Evidence-safe AI user research deserves the third validation test because the workflow term is growing much more strongly than the PM role term.
- The remaining candidates cannot be fairly compared from the broad chart because their values were compressed by the brand terms.
- No candidate passes the volume gate on Trends data. Absolute monthly searches are still required.
