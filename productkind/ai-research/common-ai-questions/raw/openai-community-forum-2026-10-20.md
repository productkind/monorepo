## Context worth knowing for the workshop

The dropped topics still say something about the tools in October 2026. ChatGPT now has separate Chat and Work modes, a feature called Dots, Codex inside the desktop app, and new model names (GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol) that change every few months. Many paying users complain that a model they relied on was retired or felt worse after an update. A toolkit item that names specific models or menus will date within weeks.

Two pricing threads come from India and Indonesia (requests for free student access, failed payments for a free trial offer). Cost and access are live questions outside the UK and US.
