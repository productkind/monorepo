# Women Build AI (Skool): what members ask

Read 20 October 2026 in Kinga's signed-in Chrome session on `skool.com/women-ai`. Read only: no votes, likes, comments or posts.

**Privacy.** Women Build AI is a members-only community and this repository is public. This file holds counts and paraphrased themes only: no member names, no verbatim posts or titles, no post links. The post-level data stayed in the browser and was not saved.

## Method

- **Coverage.** The community had 6,616 posts and 6,801 members. The post feed was read through Skool's own page-data endpoint, 30 posts a page, about two seconds apart. Skool's CDN started refusing requests at page 160 (as it did in August at about page 185), so the pull stopped there: **4,773 posts, 72% of the community**, created between 22 October 2025 and 20 October 2026.
- **By category:** Intro 1,515, General discussion 1,043, Building 692, Wins 661, Events 229, Opportunities 171, Marketing 159, Accountability 137, Fun 126, Announcements 40.
- **Question filter.** Introductions, events, announcements and wins were left out. A post counted as a question if its title had a question mark or opened with a question word ("how", "which", "anyone", "help", "advice" and so on), or if its first 400 characters asked one ("does anyone", "any tips", "should I"). That gave **739 question posts**.
- **Themes.** Each question post was matched against keyword patterns for the same families as the main report, plus builder-specific ones. A post can match several themes. Counts are keyword matches, so read them as rough proportions, not exact figures.

## The questions, paraphrased

**Which tool, and switching.** Among the most-discussed questions of the year that weren't community-wide mega threads. Members ask whether to move from ChatGPT to Claude or back, then hear a third tool is better and ask again. Within one company's products they ask which one to use (the chat app, a desktop agent for everyday work, or a coding agent). First-time app builders ask which app builder to start with. Several ask how many AI subscriptions others pay for.

**Cost and limits.** Running out of tokens or credits mid-project, an app builder using up credits faster than expected, plans changing or being downgraded, and how to make a subscription last the month.

**Consistency.** "Is it just me, or has it got worse this week?" Models being retired and brought back, answers that change from week to week, and chats that disappear.

**Context, which is where prompting goes next.** Builders ask less about writing a single prompt and more about giving the AI the right background: what to put in profile preferences, how to keep instruction files current, how to keep several clients or businesses separate, and whether a poor result is the tool's fault or a matter of what they gave it. Image edits that change people's faces come up repeatedly.

**Getting started without a technical background.** Where to start; what "building" even means for someone non-technical; whether to go away and learn AI before joining; words like Git and SEO that cause panic; and "I have a demo working on my computer, how do I make it real?".

**Privacy and security.** Where to keep confidential or health data on a small budget, how safe it is to connect AI to email and files, what happens to API keys, and how to answer clients who object to AI on privacy grounds.

**Thinking for yourself.** Threads on whether AI is making us think less and how to use it to think better, on what members won't use AI for, and on handling "AI slop" comments from people who dislike AI.

**Jobs and careers.** Tech salary ranges in Europe (one of the most-commented questions of the year), finding remote AI work, which certifications are worth it, not knowing what job title fits the skills, and how companies are rolling AI out. Nearly a quarter of introductions mention a career change or job.

**Overwhelm.** Feeling excited and overwhelmed at once, and that everything is moving too fast to keep up. One introduction in nine says so.

**Environment.** Almost absent: 8 of 739 question posts.
