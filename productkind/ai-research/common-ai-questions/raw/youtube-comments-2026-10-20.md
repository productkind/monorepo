# YouTube comments on the most-watched beginner AI videos

Pulled 20 October 2026 in Chrome on youtube.com. For nine searches (`how to use chatgpt for beginners`, `how to prompt ai`, `ai for beginners`, `how to use ai at work`, `will ai take my job`, `claude ai for beginners`, `chatgpt vs claude vs gemini`, `how to learn ai`, `what is an ai agent`), the three most-viewed results were taken, giving 23 videos. For each, the first five pages of top comments were read through YouTube's own comment endpoint: 2,134 comments in total, top-level and replies.

Comments were filtered to question-shaped ones (a question mark, or opening with a question word) between 25 and 450 characters: 150. Most of those are praise ("What a great video") or jokes. The ones below are real questions or concerns, kept verbatim with their like counts. No usernames were recorded.

**Yield: moderate.** Comment sections on these videos are dominated by thanks, creator promotion and, under the jobs videos, arguments about billionaires. Real questions are rare and low-liked, so read them as themes, not rankings.
