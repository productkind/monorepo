# Google "People also ask": 24 seed searches, UK and US

Pulled 20 October 2026 in a signed-in Chrome session on google.com, `hl=en`, `gl=gb` and `gl=us`. Each results page was fetched in page context and the questions read from the `data-q` attributes, which hold the first four or five questions Google shows before you expand the box. Fetches were spaced 2.5 to 4 seconds apart.

Google showed its "unusual traffic" check after 46 of the 48 searches, so the US versions of `what is an llm` and `how does chatgpt work` are missing. The planned second level (each question re-searched for its own box) was not run.

Verbatim questions, so spelling and punctuation are left as written. Some boxes repeat the search itself as a question; those are kept as Google showed them.
