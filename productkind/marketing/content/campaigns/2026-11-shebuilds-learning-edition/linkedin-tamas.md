# LinkedIn (Tamas): SheBuilds: Learning Edition

"A ship in port is safe, but that's not what ships are built for. Sail out to sea and do new things." This is my favourite Grace Hopper quote.

So I was sad to hear that the Grace Hopper Celebration, the big conference for women in computing, was cancelled this year, two weeks before it was due to start.

Lovable stepped in with SheBuilds: Learning Edition, a week of virtual sessions with the Lovable team, running right now until Friday 13 November. Thank you, Ashley Olafsen and everyone at Lovable, for putting it on.

We wanted to do our best to help people sail out too. Kinga and I run Little Parrot, where we teach non-technical women to build their app ideas with AI, so we've opened up the second mission of our self-paced course, Build an App with Lovable That People Pay For. The first of its seven missions is always free, and the second is a big part of the course, because by the end of it you have a working first version of your app.

In the first mission, you write down the problem your app solves and who has it, check it's worth solving, and only then write your first prompt.

In the second, you give Lovable that prompt and build the first version: you save data, add Google login and publish it on a link you can share.
