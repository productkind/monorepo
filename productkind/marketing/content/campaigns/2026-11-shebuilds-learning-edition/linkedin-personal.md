# LinkedIn - personal (Kinga, founder voice)

SheBuilds Learning Edition: Mission 2 (Build it) of Build an App with Lovable That People Pay For is free until Friday 13 November 2026, alongside Mission 1, which is always free. Job: conversion. Format: personal update.

---

Last year I was one of 215 women in the first SheBuilds on Lovable. This season I applied mainly to connect with others, and now I want to build again.

To be honest, it's the participants' fault. 😁

Several women in the SheBuilds Slack channel have written that their careers haven't been classic or linear, and they want to build an interactive CV that shows their skills better. Lots of people are doing things they've never done before, and it's hard to watch that without wanting to join in.

Many of them have also never used Lovable. So we've made the second mission of our Lovable course free this week too, on top of the first mission, which is always free.

Those two missions are the ones I'd point anyone to who's about to open Lovable for the first time.

In the first, you turn your app idea into a plan you can build: who it's for, what problem it solves, and slice our steps small enough that you don't get overwhelmed.

In the second, you build it one focused prompt at a time, revert to an earlier version when you don't like a change, and publish it online so you can share it.

And even if you never open the course, cut your app down to the one thing your user needs to do in it before you write your first prompt. A small first version is easier for Lovable to build the way you imagined, and easier for you to test and improve. That's how I'm starting my new one too.

If you're part of SheBuilds, or you've been meaning to try Lovable, both missions are free until Monday 16 November: https://littleparrot.app/courses/build-an-app-with-lovable-that-people-pay-for?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=k&utm_campaign=shebuilds-learning-edition&utm_content=first-sb
