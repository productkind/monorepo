Last year I was one of 215 women in the first SheBuilds on Lovable. This season I applied just to watch, and now I want to build again.

To be honest, it's the participants' fault 😅 [One real thing you've seen someone do this season.] Lots of people are doing things they've never done before, and it's hard to watch that without wanting to join in.

Many of them have also never used Lovable. So we've made the second mission of our Lovable course free this week too, on top of the first mission, which is always free.

Those two missions are the ones I'd point anyone to who's about to open Lovable for the first time. In the first, you write down the problem your app solves and who has it, and turn it into your first prompt. In the second, you build it one focused prompt at a time, revert to an earlier version when you don't like a change, and publish it on a link you can share.

And even if you never open the course, cut your app down to the one thing your user needs to do in it before you write your first prompt. A small first version is easier for Lovable to get right, and easier for you to fix when something breaks. That's how I'm starting my new one too: [what you're thinking of building, or honestly, that you don't know yet].
