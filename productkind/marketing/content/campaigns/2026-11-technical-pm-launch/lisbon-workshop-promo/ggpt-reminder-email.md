# Geek Girls reminder email (plain text, sent by hand)

Send to: Geek Girls subscriber list (Portuguese community for women in tech), on Thursday 12 November.

Follows `ggpt-email.md` (sent 29 October). No one registered from that email, though many people viewed the Luma page, so this one says up front that the workshop works for anyone who works with developers, not only PMs. Those numbers are background only and stay out of the copy.

Luma can only record utm_source, so this email uses `utm_source=ggpt-reminder` to tell its sign-ups apart from the first email's `ggpt`.

Anyone who registers after the setup email went out needs the setup steps sent by hand (`../lisbon-workshop-reminder-email.md` has them).

Subject: Tomorrow a free, hands-on workshop on how web apps work

Hi everyone,

I hope you're all safe and well after Storm Charles.

A quick reminder that our free workshop, How web apps work, is this Friday at PTW Palace in Lisbon.

We designed it with product managers in mind, but it's also useful for other roles who work with developers but don't have an engineering background themselves: designers, business analysts, product owners, project managers, and anyone else who has conversations where a deeper technical understanding could help.

You'll run a web app in a development environment, track down why it doesn't save what you enter, and give the evidence to an AI agent to fix it. Afterwards, you'll be able to follow technical discussions with more confidence, and ask developers better questions.

🗓️ When: Friday 13 November, 19:00 to 21:30

📍 Where: PTW Palace, Rua Gomes Freire 98, Lisbon

🎟️ Cost and language: Free, in English

Bring a laptop. We'll have treats from Gleba, and you can buy drinks at the Palace bar.

You can [register for the workshop here](https://luma.com/0hccxsn4?utm_source=ggpt-reminder).

If you know someone who works with developers but don't have a technical background, please share this with them. I'd love to see you there!

Cheers, Kinga
