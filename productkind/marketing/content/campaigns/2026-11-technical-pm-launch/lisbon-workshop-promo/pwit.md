**Title:** Free workshop in Lisbon on 13 November: how web apps work, for product managers

Hi everyone 👋

If you're a product manager without an engineering background, you still need to join technical discussions and judge how complex a change is. Several product managers told me a deeper technical understanding would give them more confidence when they talk to developers, so Tamas Kokeny and I are running a free, hands-on workshop on how web apps work during Portugal Tech Week.

In the workshop, you'll:

- run a real app in your own development environment, which opens in your browser, so there's nothing to install;
- follow what happens when a customer clicks Save, through the browser, the server and the database, and find out why their new record never appears;
- write a bug report with the evidence an engineer needs, then give it to GitHub Copilot, an AI agent, and test its fix.

The next time an engineer explains why something isn't working, you'll know which questions to ask: did the request reach the server, what does the server log say, and did anything get saved in the database?

I'll be honest: so far only men have signed up, and none of them are product managers (they're founders and investors). So please don't rule yourself out. If it could help in your work, or you're curious and think it could help you build with AI, come along.

📍 PTW Palace, Rua Gomes Freire 98, Lisbon

🗓️ Friday 13 November, 19:00 to 21:30

It's free and in English. Bring a laptop and a free, personal GitHub account; we'll email you the setup steps beforehand. We'll have snacks, and you can buy drinks at the Palace bar.
