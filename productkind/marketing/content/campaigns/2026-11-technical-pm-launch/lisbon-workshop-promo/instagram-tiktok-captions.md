## Video hook notes

The Reel is a still, so frame one is the whole hook, and the image does that job well: the yellow title names the audience and the topic, and the faces are real. Two things for the edit:

- Keep the Reel short (about 6 to 8 seconds). Reels distribution runs on full watch-through, and a still has nothing new to show after the viewer has read it.
- Use a light, slow zoom onto the title or the photo so it doesn't read as a frozen screen, and a quiet track from Instagram's audio library.
