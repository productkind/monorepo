**Title:** Tomorrow in Lisbon: a free, hands-on workshop on how web apps work

This is a quick reminder, if you're not yet tired of events after Web Summit 😄, tomorrow we're running a free, hands-on workshop at PTW Palace: How web apps work.

You'll run a web app, track down why it doesn't save what you enter, and give the evidence to an AI agent to fix. Our hope is this workshop will help you participate in technical discussions with more confidence.

We designed it with PMs in mind, but if you work with developers without an engineering background as a designer, business analyst or in another role, you're very welcome too.

Bring a laptop, and we'll have treats from Gleba.

You can register here: https://luma.com/0hccxsn4?utm_source=pwit-reminder

I’d love to see many of you there!
