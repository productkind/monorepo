**How web apps work: a free, hands-on workshop for product managers**

As a product manager, you work closely with engineers and make decisions that involve technical trade-offs, often without an engineering background yourself. Explore how web apps work under the surface, so you're better equipped to understand technical discussions about your own product, ask about the details that affect your decisions and have productive conversations with engineers.

At this workshop, you'll run a real open-source CRM yourself and get a message from sales: a customer tried to add a new company, the Save button kept spinning, and the company never appeared in their list. As you investigate the root cause, you'll see how the app processes the information you enter, where it checks that information, and how a failure in one layer affects what you see in the browser. At the end, you'll give your evidence to an AI agent, let it fix the bug and check that the fix works.

Your hosts are Kinga Magyar, a lead product manager who's still learning new technical details after more than eight years in product, and Tamas Kokeny, an engineering leader, from LittleParrot.app.
