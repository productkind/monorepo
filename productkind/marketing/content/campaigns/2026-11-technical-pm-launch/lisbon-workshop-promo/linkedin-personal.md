Three people have signed up for our workshop for product managers in Lisbon. All three are men, and none of them are product managers.

They're founders and investors. I'd love to see more women in the room, and some product managers too.

Tamas and I decided to run it because several product managers told me the same thing: a deeper technical understanding would make them more confident talking to developers and managing stakeholder expectations.

So we built two hours around one bug. A customer clicks Save, the app spins, and their record never appears.

How do you find out where it broke? You'll run Twenty, a real open-source CRM, in your own development environment (it opens in your browser, so there's nothing to install) and follow that failed save through the browser, the server and the database. Then you'll write a bug report with the evidence an engineer needs, give it to GitHub Copilot and test its fix.

The next time an engineer explains why something isn't working, you'll know which questions to ask: did the request reach the server, what does the server log say, and did anything get saved in the database?

You don't need an engineering background, and you won't write code yourself. So please don't rule yourself out, especially if you're a woman in product. And if you're not a product manager but it could help you at work, or you're curious and think it could help you build with AI, come along too.

It's on Friday 13 November at 19:00, at PTW Palace in Lisbon, as part of Portugal Tech Week. It's free and in English. Bring a laptop, and we'll have snacks.
