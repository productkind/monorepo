We designed our Portugal Tech Week workshop for product managers, and so far the biggest group signed up is startup founders.

I find that interesting, because as a founder I've needed the same technical understanding I needed as a product manager.

In an early-stage startup, the founder is often the product manager too, whether the engineering team is people or an AI agent. You translate the customers' problems into what gets built.

The workshop is two hours of hands-on exploration. You'll run a real open-source CRM in a development environment, in which you'll investigate a bug we added for you to get practical experience in the software.

You'll leave understanding how a web app works end-to-end, and our hope is you'll gain more confidence in technical discussions. So the next time a conversation with your engineers, or your AI agent, gets technical, you'll know what to ask next.

The workshop is free, on Friday 13 November at PTW Palace in Lisbon. Bring a laptop. I'd especially love to see women product managers and founders there.
