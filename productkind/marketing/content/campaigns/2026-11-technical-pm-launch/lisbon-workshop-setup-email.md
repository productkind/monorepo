**Subject:** 🦜 Get your laptop ready for Friday's workshop

Hi there,

Thank you for signing up for How web apps work! We're really glad you're coming, and we're looking forward to meeting you on Friday 13 November at PTW Palace, Rua Gomes Freire 98, Lisbon. We'll start at 19:00.

In the workshop, you'll run a real app in your own development environment in GitHub Codespaces, which opens in your browser. Before Friday, please take about 15 minutes to set up a free GitHub account and check that your laptop can open the tools we'll use. None of it needs any technical knowledge, and if something doesn't work, the end of this email says what to do.

1. Create a free GitHub account

Sign up at https://github.com/signup. Use a personal account, not one your employer gives you: GitHub Copilot's free plan doesn't work on accounts an employer manages.

2. Turn on GitHub Copilot Free

Go to https://github.com/settings/copilot. If you see a button to start using Copilot Free, click it. You're set up when the page says "GitHub Copilot Free" at the top.

On the same page, under Privacy, set "Allow GitHub to use my data for AI model training" to Disabled, so GitHub doesn't train its AI models on what you type.

3. Check that your laptop can open a codespace

Go to https://github.com/codespaces/templates, find Blank (usually the first card) and click "Use this template". A new tab opens with VS Code, a code editor, running in your browser. If the editor opens within a couple of minutes, Codespaces works on your laptop.

Then delete it, so it doesn't use your free hours: go to https://github.com/codespaces, click the three dots next to your codespace, then Delete.

You don't need to do anything else with Codespaces before Friday. We'll create the codespace for the workshop together on the day.

4. Check that you can open the developer tools

Open any website, then open the developer tools in your browser:

- Google Chrome: press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) on Windows, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac. You can also click the three dots at the top right, then More tools, then Developer tools.
- Microsoft Edge: press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) on Windows, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac. You can also click the three dots at the top right, then More tools, then Developer tools.
- Firefox: press F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+I) on Windows, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac. You can also click the three lines at the top right, then More tools, then Web Developer Tools.
- Safari: first go to Safari, then Settings, then Advanced, and tick "Show features for web developers". Then press Cmd+Option+I.

It works if a panel opens with tabs such as Console and Network.

Do steps 3 and 4 on the laptop and in the browser you'll bring on Friday. Some company laptops block one or both, even on a different wifi network.

If a check doesn't work

That's fine, and it happens with some company laptops. If step 3 or 4 doesn't work on a work laptop, please bring a personal laptop if you can. If you can't, please come anyway: you can pair up with someone on the day and work through the whole workshop together.

On Friday, bring your laptop and its charger. We'll have snacks too.

We can't wait to see you on Friday!

Kinga, Tamas & Little Parrot 💛
