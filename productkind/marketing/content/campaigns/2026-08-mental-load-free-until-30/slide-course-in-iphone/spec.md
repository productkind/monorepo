# Spec: single slide, the course build in an iPhone frame

Part of the [Hand Off the Mental Load, free until 30 August](../brief.md)
campaign. One 1080x1350 slide for Instagram, not a carousel. Built with the
`.phone` iPhone mockup in `productkind/carousel-design/`.

## The slide

| Element | Text (verbatim) |
| --- | --- |
| Headline | Hand Off the Mental Load |
| Sub | Turn a spoken brain dump into a list your partner gets automatically |
| Footnote line 1 | Two iPhone voice shortcuts for mums |
| Footnote line 2 | Build the first one in 6 minutes on |
| Badge | LittleParrot.app |
| Offer line | Free until 30 August |

The headline highlight (`.hl`) sits on **Mental Load**. The headline is the
first half of the course title, so it keeps its title case; the sub is our own
line and is sentence case. Both are set in Space Mono, as is the offer line
under the badge, which carries the gradient highlight across it.

The sub carries the whole mechanism, including the part the screenshots cannot
show: the list reaches her partner with no extra step. It sits on two lines,
broken after "list". That is true on both routes the course teaches, the shared Mental
Load folder for a partner on an iPhone (it updates live on their phone), and
the message the shortcut sends each run for a partner on any other phone.

## The phones

Two iPhone frames side by side with a curly orange arrow between them, so the
slide reads left to right: what she said, then what her phone wrote. The arrow
is the brand orange, hand-drawn looking and level, with a hook curl in its tail.

- **Phone 1:** the brain dump being spoken, the shortcut's dictation panel open
  over the home screen. The full screenshot, aligned to the frame.
- **Phone 2:** the shared Food & Household note, the full screenshot aligned to
  the frame, with the other two lists stacked underneath its last line: the
  Logistics list, then the Misc list, each a full-width crop of its own
  screenshot from the same phone. Because every piece is a full-width crop at
  the same scale, the text lines up pixel for pixel and the three lists read as
  one neat note.

Screenshot text is inside the images, so it carries no spec coverage. Both
screenshots bring their own status bar, dynamic island and Notes toolbar, so the
component's drawn `.statusbar` stays off.
