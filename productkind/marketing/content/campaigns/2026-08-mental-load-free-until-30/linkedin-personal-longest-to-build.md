Out of our nine courses, the shortest one took us the longest to build.

“Hand Off the Mental Load: Use AI to Turn a Brain Dump into a List” is a 20-minute course we built especially for mums. We made it free until 30 August so more mums can benefit from it.

You build two shortcuts. One automates sorting a brain dump into neat lists, and sends the lists to your partner. Our hope is that it can help to make that invisible labour easier to see and share. The other creates a baby log where you can track how much your baby ate, slept, and any important milestones.

You can build these with the Shortcuts app on your iPhone.

Developing this course gave us a lot of headache.

We use AI to help with research when we design our hands-on courses. But documentation can be outdated, incomplete or simply incorrect. So we build and test every step by hand before we publish a course and it reaches our learners, because no one has time for unclear build instructions. 

Every time I thought we were done with a challenge, I kept finding another edge case. An empty dictation could make the AI invent a list. One setting could send the wrong response into the next action. Dictation sometimes stopped when I took a breath. So we reworked the course several times to make it bulletproof.

I really hope it will be valuable for many caregivers. Our first learners found it useful.
