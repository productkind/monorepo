# Tech Women Summit Moldova raffle: LinkedIn posts

Posts entered in the "Fold your Little Parrot, and win!" raffle at Tech Women Summit Moldova, Chișinău, Saturday 24 October 2026. The flyer and folding page are in [`origami-crane/`](origami-crane/), and the rules are on [littleparrot.app/tech-women-moldova](https://littleparrot.app/tech-women-moldova).

**To enter:** a public LinkedIn post with a photo of the folded parrot that tags Little Parrot, posted by 15:00 Chișinău time (EEST) on 24 October.

**The draw:** three winners, drawn in the last hour of the summit and announced on LinkedIn. Each wins a free place on Launch Your App Idea with Lovable (worth €299).

Posting times come from the post ID (its first 41 bits are the creation time), so they ignore later edits.
