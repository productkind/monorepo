Fold your Little Parrot

Your flyer is a 10 cm square, and with the folds below it becomes a parrot: orange head, green tail, an eye on one side and a wink on the other. Every step has an animation that loops, so you can watch it as many times as you need.

Before you start

  • Work on a hard, flat table and run a fingernail along every crease. Sharp creases make the later steps much easier.
  • The inside reverse fold in steps 7 and 8 is the trickiest part. Pre-crease the line first by folding the point over both ways, then open the layers and push the point in along that crease.
  • If a step goes wrong, unfold it and try again. The creases you already made are still there to guide you.

How to fold it

  1. Step 1

    Crease it in half both ways

    Start with the colourful side face down, so you see the dashed fold lines. Fold the sheet in half edge to edge and unfold it. Then fold it in half the other way and unfold it again.

  2. Step 2

    Crease the diagonals

    Turn the sheet over so the colours face you. Fold it in half corner to corner and unfold, then do the same with the other two corners.

  3. Step 3

    Collapse it into a small square

    Push the centre down gently and bring all four corners together at the bottom. The creases do most of the work. Flatten it with the colours on the outside and the loose corners pointing down. The corner on top should be a plain one, with no eye and no writing. If it isn’t, swing one flap across like turning a page.

  4. Step 4

    Make three guide creases

    Fold the lower edges of the top layer in to the centre line. Fold the small triangle at the top down over them. Crease firmly, then unfold all three.

  5. Step 5

    Lift the top layer up

    Lift the bottom corner of the top layer and fold it up along the crease you made under the triangle. The sides fold inwards along the other two creases. Flatten it into a long diamond.

  6. Step 6

    Repeat on the back

    Turn it over and repeat the last two steps on this side. The two loose points at the bottom become the neck and the tail.

  7. Step 7

    Fold up the neck and the tail

    Look at the two bottom points: the one with the eye on it is the neck. Inside reverse fold each point up between the wings: open its layers a little, push the point up inside along a slanted crease, then flatten it.

  8. Step 8

    Fold the head

    Inside reverse fold the tip of the neck down, about 1.5 cm from the end, just above the eye.

  9. Step 9

    Fold the wings down

    Fold the front wing down along its base, where it meets the body, then fold the back wing down the same way. Hold the base of the neck and the tail and pull gently to spread the wings. One side winks, the other one is looking at you. Lift the wing on that side to find an arrow pointing to the QR code on top.
