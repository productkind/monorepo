# You asked for one button

You asked for one button. The AI changed fourteen files.

The AI says it's complete. The page still loads in preview. Do you publish it?

One of those files could control sign-in, payments or how customer data is
saved. You can't judge the change by looking at the new button.

You need to know what changed, which customer journeys could be affected and
what to test before release.

If you want that control over your own app, that's what we're building a
learning path for.

By the end, you'll review a proposed change, test the affected behaviour and
decide whether to release it or restore the working version.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
