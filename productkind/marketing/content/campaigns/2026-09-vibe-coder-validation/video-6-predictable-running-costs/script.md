# Could one customer increase your bill?

Could one customer increase your bill?

Your AI app builder, database, email service and AI model all charge differently.
Some costs stay fixed. Others rise with usage.

That makes a cheap prototype hard to price as a live product.

You need to know what one active customer costs, what happens when ten become a
hundred and which service will charge you first.

If you want to understand the cost of running your app, that's what we're
building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll list your fixed and usage costs, estimate a cost per active
customer, set spending alerts and test whether your price can cover them.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
