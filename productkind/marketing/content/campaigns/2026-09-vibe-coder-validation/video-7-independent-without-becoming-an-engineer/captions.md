**Caption:**

Running the app you built with AI still requires decisions the AI cannot make for you.

A customer cannot sign in. A payment fails. A change breaks another screen. The AI proposes a fix, and you decide what happens next.

Use the same routine each time so you can check a proposed change before publishing it:

- Keep a simple map of the app and the services it uses.
- Save and name a working version.
- Record the exact problem and expected behaviour.
- Review which parts the proposed change affects.
- Test the main customer journey before publishing.
- Restore the working version when a test fails.

We’re building this learning path for women who have already built a product with AI and want to handle updates and problems themselves.
