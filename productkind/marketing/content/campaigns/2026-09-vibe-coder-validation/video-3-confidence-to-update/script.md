# Scared to touch your own app?

Scared to touch your app that you built with AI?

You want to change one onboarding screen. The AI starts editing login,
the database and files you have never seen before.

So you cancel the change. The improvement stays on your list.

Updating a live product needs a working version you can return to and a way to
check what the change might break.

If you want to keep improving your app without relying on luck, that's what
we're building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll save a working version, make a small scoped change, test the
affected journey and undo it if the test fails.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
