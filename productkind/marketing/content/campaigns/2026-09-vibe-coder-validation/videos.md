# Video scripts

Eight outcome-led videos for the Building Apps with AI waitlist campaign. Each script is designed for roughly 45 seconds of narration and follows the same shape as the technical Product Manager validation videos: a recognisable situation, its consequence, the capability the learner is missing, the learning-path response, a concrete outcome, the waitlist call to action and a question that invites comments.

- **Brand:** Little Parrot
- **Audience:** Women who have already built a working app with AI and want to prepare it for customers, payments and ongoing changes
- **Channels:** LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
- **Destination:** `https://littleparrot.app/guides/building-apps-with-ai`
- **Format:** Narration with burned-in subtitles and changing screen recordings
- **Timing:** Approximately 45 seconds at 2.95 spoken words per second

## The eight scripts

| # | Script | Outcome |
| --: | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Your app works. Customers get stuck.](video-1-real-product-not-prototype/script.md) | A real product, not just a prototype |
| 2 | [You asked for one button](video-2-control-what-ai-changes/script.md) | Control over what the AI is doing |
| 3 | [Scared to touch your own app?](video-3-confidence-to-update/script.md) | Confidence to update the product |
| 4 | [Would you take a payment today?](video-4-customer-data-and-money/script.md) | Safety around customers, data and money |
| 5 | [Your app goes down on Saturday](video-5-keep-the-product-operable/script.md) | A product that remains operable |
| 6 | [Could one customer increase your bill?](video-6-predictable-running-costs/script.md) | Predictable costs |
| 7 | [You launched. Three people visited.](video-7-revenue-and-validation/script.md) | Revenue and validation |
| 8 | [Can you run your own app?](video-8-independent-without-becoming-an-engineer/script.md) | Independence without pretending to be an engineer |

The situations are reformulated from the audience research rather than quoted from individual participants. The scripts promise only the checks, decisions and working practices described on the waitlist page. They do not promise that every app will become secure, compliant, profitable or ready for unrestricted use.
