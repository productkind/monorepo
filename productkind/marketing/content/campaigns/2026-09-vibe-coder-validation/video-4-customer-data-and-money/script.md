# Would you take a payment today?

Would you take a payment today?

What is that customer trusting you with? Can one customer see
another person's data? Are private keys visible in the browser? Does checkout
charge the right amount and send the right confirmation?

Then there are backups, cookie consent, terms and a privacy policy.

You need practical checks and clear signs that tell you when the app needs a
security or legal professional.

If you want to handle customer data and money responsibly, that's what we're
building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll test access, permissions, payments and backups, and record
the risks that still need specialist help.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
