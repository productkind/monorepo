## LinkedIn

**Caption:**

Before your AI-built app accepts a customer’s data or payment, test what you are asking them to trust you with.

Start with five checks:

1. Can one customer see or change another customer’s data?
2. Are private keys or credentials exposed in the browser?
3. Does checkout charge the correct amount and send confirmation?
4. Can you restore important information from a backup?
5. Do your privacy policy, terms and cookie consent reflect what the app does?

These checks cannot certify every app as secure or compliant. They help you find risks, correct what you can and recognise questions that require professional advice.
