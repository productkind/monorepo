customer reached checkout, paid, and couldn't tell what would happen next.

You built the core feature. The full customer journey still has gaps.

payment, email and the result you promised.

a learning path for.

By the end, you'll map the main journey, test every step and common failure,
and create a launch checklist for the parts that still need work.
