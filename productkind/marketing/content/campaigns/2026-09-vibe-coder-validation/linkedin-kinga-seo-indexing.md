If you built your website or app with AI, this is the first SEO task I’d do:

**Check whether Google has actually indexed your important pages.**

Because you can have the best-written page in your niche...

...and get zero traffic from Google if the page isn't in its index.

Luckily, this is easy to check:

1. If you haven’t already, set up **Google Search Console** for your website. It’s free.

2. Open **URL Inspection** and paste in one of your important URLs.

3. Look for: **“URL is on Google.”**

If it isn’t, investigate why. Common things to check:

→ an accidental `noindex` tag  
→ `robots.txt` blocking the page  
→ incorrect canonical URLs  
→ missing internal links  
→ the page missing from your sitemap

And if you built your site with AI, give your coding agent this prompt:

> Audit my website for SEO indexing problems. Check that all important public pages are crawlable and indexable by Google. Look for noindex directives, robots.txt blocks, incorrect canonical URLs, broken internal links and pages missing from the sitemap. Don't change anything yet. List the affected URLs and recommend how to fix each issue.

Once you’ve fixed the problems, go back to Search Console and **request indexing**.

One important distinction:

Being indexed doesn’t mean Google will rank your page.

Think of indexing as getting through the front door.
