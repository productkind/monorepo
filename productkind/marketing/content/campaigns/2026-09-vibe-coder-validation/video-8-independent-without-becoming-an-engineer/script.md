# Can you run your own app?

Can you run your own app?

You built it with AI. Then a customer can't sign in, a payment fails, or a
change breaks another screen.

The AI gives you an answer. You decide whether to publish the fix, restore the
working version or collect more evidence.

Owning your app means knowing its main parts, keeping a working version and
checking the main customer journey.

If you want a repeatable way to run your app yourself, that's what we're
building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll map your app, review changes, test the customer journey,
restore a working version and choose your next step using evidence you
collected.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
