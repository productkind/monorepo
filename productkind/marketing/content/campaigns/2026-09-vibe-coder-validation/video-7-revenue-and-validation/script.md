# You launched. Three people visited.

You launched your app. Three people visited, and nobody paid.

Now you don't know which problem to solve. Do you need more visitors, a clearer
offer, an easier sign-up or a different price?

Adding another feature cannot answer that question. Customer conversations and
behaviour can.

You need one hypothesis, one result to measure and the smallest test that could
change your next decision.

If you want evidence that your product solves a worthwhile problem, that's
what we're building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll track a meaningful customer action, run a focused
experiment, test a price and give real customers a working way to pay.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.

charge?
