# Your app goes down on Saturday

The app you built with AI goes down on Saturday. Who notices it first?

A live app needs ongoing care. A service can fail, a platform can change or
your next update can break the live version.

If the only recovery plan is asking the AI to fix it, every outage starts with
guessing.

You need alerts, a tested backup and a recovery checklist you can follow
yourself.

If you want an app you can keep operating after launch, that's what we're
building a learning path for.

By the end, you'll set an availability alert, practise restoring a working
version and plan your updates, backups and cost checks.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
