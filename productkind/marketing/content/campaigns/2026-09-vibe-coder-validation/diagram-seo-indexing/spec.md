# Diagram: getting into Google's index

Two panels, same two actors, same arrow. In panel one the page gets into the index and Search Console says so. In panel two a saw-tooth breaks the arrow part way, and the five things that put it there are listed underneath. The title names the outcome a reader cares about, being discoverable, rather than the mechanism.

## Slide 1 (the only slide)

Visual: two framed panels. Each has a document icon labelled your page on the left, a database icon labelled Google's index on the right, and one arrow between them. Panel two's arrow is greyed and broken by a saw-tooth before it arrives. Branding sits in the bottom left corner, with the Little Parrot mascot's head peeking over the bottom edge of the card beside it.

- Title: The first SEO check: is your page discoverable?
- Footer handle: LittleParrot.app

### Panel 1

- Kicker, on a gradient highlight: IN THE INDEX
- Left actor: your page
- Right actor: Google's index
- Arrow, left to right, solid. Tag: what happens. Payload: crawled and indexed
- Outcome label: SEARCH CONSOLE SHOWS
- Outcome box: URL is on Google.

### Panel 2

- Kicker, on a gradient highlight: NOT IN THE INDEX
- Left actor: your page
- Right actor: Google's index
- Arrow, left to right, greyed and broken by a saw-tooth part way. Tag: what happens. Payload: blocked before it gets in
- Outcome label: FIVE THINGS TO CHECK
- Outcome list, one per line:
  - an accidental noindex tag
  - robots.txt blocking the page
  - incorrect canonical URLs
  - missing internal links
  - the page missing from your sitemap

## Takeaway

A page that is not in the index cannot rank, whatever is written on it, and the five usual causes are all things you can check yourself.
