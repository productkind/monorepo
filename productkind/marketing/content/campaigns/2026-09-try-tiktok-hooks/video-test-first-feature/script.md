## Rest of the video

Let me explain. [pause]

You write it all down first, a PRD, a spec, one long prompt, and give the lot to your AI app builder. It builds something. It runs. It's just not what you pictured.

It always gives you something back, and never says which parts it guessed.

So give it one thing at a time. Say your first one is this: someone picks a time and sees it booked. Nothing else.

Then compare what came back with what was in your head. Does it behave the way you imagined? Is that how you'd word it? Has it added fields nobody asked for?

Because it adds. A settings page, a login screen, a colour scheme you never mentioned. Take them out.

One feature at a time is the only size where you can still see the difference.

[long pause][curious] What did your AI app builder build that you never asked for?
