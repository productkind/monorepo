# LinkedIn (Kinga): "I'm technologically challenged"

Just this week, I heard several women say some version of: "I'm technologically challenged."

I understand where it comes from, but I also see how that one sentence can stop an idea before the first prompt, whether it's a website for our business, a portfolio, or a digital tool that could help us at work.

So what changes it? To be honest, I don't know a shortcut. The only way I know to get more confident is to practise: try something, see what breaks, fix it, and try the next thing.

And that's why I'd love more women to apply to SheBuilds, Lovable's hackathon for women. You'd be practising alongside lots of other women building at the same time and, if you get in, with A LOT of Lovable credits to experiment with. You don't need a paid plan to apply. I was one of 215 women accepted in the first season in October 2025 (about 3,000 applied), so I know not everyone gets a place, but it's worth a try.
