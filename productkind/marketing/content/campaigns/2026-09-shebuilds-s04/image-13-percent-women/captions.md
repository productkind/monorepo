## Video hook notes

The video is made from the still image, so the notes are about how to reveal it:

- **Frame one**: show only the first half of the stat ("80% of people building with Lovable come from non-technical backgrounds"), so there's a question in the first second: who are they?
- **Around 0:01 to 0:02**: reveal "But only 13% are women." This is the contradiction, and it lands before most viewers have scrolled.
- **Then**: "Let's change that!", then the course card with "Free until 18 October". Hold the course card longest (3 to 4 seconds), so the URL is readable.
- Keep it short (about 7 to 9 seconds) so it loops cleanly back to the 80% line. With no voiceover, the on-screen text is all that TikTok and YouTube can index, so the text itself carries the search terms (Lovable, women).
