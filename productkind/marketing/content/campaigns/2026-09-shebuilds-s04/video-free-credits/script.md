# SheBuilds season 4: free credits, and a free course to use them well

**Log line:** Out of Lovable credits? SheBuilds season 4 gives you free credits if you get in, and applications close on Sunday.

**Real material:**

- Kinga's draft (2026-09-24): SheBuilds on Lovable season 4 is here; it solves one of the biggest problems our learners tell us about all the time; AI app builders let non-technical people build their ideas but they often run out of credits; no paid subscription needed to apply; participants get credits during the competition; applications open until 27 September (a Sunday); to help more women get started, Build Your First App with Lovable is free until the end of the season on 18 October.
- Kinga (2026-09-24): no official credit number published for season 4 yet (earlier seasons had unlimited credits), so the script says "free credits" and never a number or "unlimited".
- Not everyone who applies gets in (S01: about 3,000 applied, 215 accepted, `productkind/little-parrot-context.md`), so credits are always "if you get in".
- The course: `little-parrot/content/course/lovable-intro-00/lovable-intro-00.yaml`. Challenge 1 teaches starting with one core feature, using Dalmie's book club app (a suggestion form first, nothing else), and that a focused prompt often uses fewer credits than a mega-prompt followed by lots of fixes. Later challenges cover iterating step by step, a real database and login, recovering from mistakes, and publishing.

## Prediction

Anchored to the TikTok review baseline: 3.8 s average watch, 0.5% full watches, 0.7 saves and shares per 1,000 views; targets 7 s, 20% and 5.

- **Average watch time:** 5 to 6 s. The hook names a specific, felt situation for our audience, and the deadline gives a reason to stay, but it's still a 38-second narrated video.
- **Watched to the end:** 2 to 4%.
- **Saves + shares per 1,000 views:** 3 to 4. Deadline news with free credits is share-shaped ("send this to a friend"), more than our teaching videos.
- **Hook we expect to win, and why:** B. "Free credits" and "Sunday" are a concrete gain on a clock in the first five words, and the text works with the sound off. A may win with people already building in Lovable, which is our core audience.
