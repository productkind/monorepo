**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** Say what the prototype is for before you demo it: "this exists to check whether people would use it, and it's built to be thrown away." Then the question afterwards tends to be what we learned rather than when we're launching. Has a demo ever got away from you?

**Thomas:** Raise real data first. A prototype usually holds whatever you typed into it, with no access rules and no backup, so the moment a real customer's details go in, it stops being a test and becomes something you have to look after.
