**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** These three are the ones we picked for the learning path. Asking them doesn't mean you're doubting your engineers, and it helps to say so out loud: "I want to check what we're taking on before I promise a date." What was the last thing that turned out bigger than anyone expected?

**Thomas:** "What happens when a request fails halfway?" changes estimates more often than the other two, because the answer decides whether the team has to build a way to undo the work. Ask it while the scope is still open and you'll hear about it before it becomes a surprise.
