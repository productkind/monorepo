# LinkedIn post: how technical knowledge accumulated across Kinga's PM career

One of the products I managed was distributed on a DVD, posted out to manufacturers.

Others ran on Azure or AWS, written in C#, Java, TypeScript. The languages and the platforms were different, but the concepts were mostly the same.

I picked those concepts up slowly. I did a four-month developer bootcamp where I learned the basics of coding, and that helped me a lot to understand how software is built. Then I worked as a developer for a year and a half, and after that I learned something new at every company I joined as a PM. Even after all that, I was still learning new technical concepts years later.

It's interesting to think how our knowledge accumulates over the years.

But you might not have years, or even months.

You might need to sit in a conversation about a technical decision with your development team this week. And manage a stakeholder who "just" asked for a new button and can't see the backend work behind it.
