**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** Reproducing it yourself feels like extra work when you're busy, and it really isn't. Before you forward anything, try it on your own account, step by step. Either you can't, which is information, or you can, and then you can write down the exact clicks that get you there. That version of the report gets picked up.

**Thomas:** Your browser records more than you'd think. Open the developer tools, go to the network tab, do the thing that fails, and you'll see the request that went red and the number next to it. A 403 and a 500 send an engineer to completely different places, and you can tell them which one it was.
