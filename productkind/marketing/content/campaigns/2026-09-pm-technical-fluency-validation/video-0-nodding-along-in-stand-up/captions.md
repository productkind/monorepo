**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** Try saying "can I say that back, to check I've understood?" Then repeat it in your own words and let them correct you. You're not asking anyone to explain it from scratch, you're checking, and that's a much easier thing to ask in a busy stand-up. Does anyone on your team already do this, or would you be the first?

**Thomas:** Ask two follow-ups: what exactly are we waiting for, and who owns it? "Blocked by the platform team" usually means somebody else has to finish or approve something before your work can move, and those two answers are what your stakeholder needs when you give them the new date.
