**Caption:**

"So how does your product work?" You can demo every screen, and that's as far as your answer goes.

Behind those screens is a service another team owns, three integrations, cloud storage you've never seen, and a database somebody migrated last year. So when the load test fails, you can't tell whether it's your problem.

You know the frontend and the backend. Nobody explains the infrastructure around them.

That's what we're building a learning path around, for women in product who want to know what actually sits behind their own screens. By the end you'll be able to map it out: what it depends on, who owns each part, where it slows down, and what breaks when one piece fails.

**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** To be honest, a drawing helps me more than any document here. Take one thing a user does, one click, and draw every box it passes through until the answer comes back. Where you have to stop and guess is the part you don't know yet, and that's a useful list to have before the next planning meeting.

**Thomas:** Worth writing a name next to each box too, because "who owns this?" is the question that unblocks you fastest when something breaks. If two boxes have the same owner and one has nobody, you've just found where the slow answers come from.
