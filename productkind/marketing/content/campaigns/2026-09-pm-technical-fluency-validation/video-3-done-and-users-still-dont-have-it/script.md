# Done, and your users still don't have it

Engineering says it's done. Your users still don't have it.

It's built, it's tested, and it's waiting behind another team's release.
Nobody told you that team ships once a month.

You've been telling your stakeholder it's coming this week. For three weeks.

Done meant finished in your part of the system. That isn't the same as live
for your users.

If you want to know where your feature actually is, we're building a learning
path for it.

By the end you'll be able to follow one change through review, testing,
environments and every team it waits on, and say exactly what's holding it
up.

The waitlist link is in the comments. Sign up and we'll let you know when the
learning path opens.
