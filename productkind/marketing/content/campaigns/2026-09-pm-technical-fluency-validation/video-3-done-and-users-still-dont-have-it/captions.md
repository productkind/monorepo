**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** "Done" is worth agreeing out loud with your team, because it means something different to each of you. Ask what has to be true before a user can see it, write that list down, and use the same list in every status update after that. What does done mean on your team?

**Thomas:** Ask which environment the change is in right now, and what moves it to the next one. There are usually only three or four steps between an engineer's laptop and your users, and once you can name them you can also name the step it's stuck on.
