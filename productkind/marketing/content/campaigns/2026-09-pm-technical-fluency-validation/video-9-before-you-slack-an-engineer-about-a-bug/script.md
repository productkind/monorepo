# Before you Slack an engineer about a bug

Product managers, stop sending engineers “I found a bug” on Slack if you want fewer questions back.

The engineer still has to ask: Where did it happen? What did you do? Can you repeat it?

Give them these seven answers in your first message.

First, try it again and say whether it happens every time.

Second, attach a screenshot or screen recording that shows the bug.

Third, list each step: Profile, Upload, Choose photo, Save.

Fourth, say what you expected: the new photo should save.

Fifth, describe what happened: the spinner kept loading.

Sixth, include your device, browser and app version.

Seventh, explain who is affected and what they cannot do.

The engineer can start investigating with fewer follow-up questions.

Save this as your bug-report template.
