**Founder comments:**

**Kinga:** Decide what the number means before you worry about writing the query. "How many people finished onboarding" needs a decision about what "finished" means and which accounts you count, and that decision is yours to make rather than the analyst's. Write it next to the number every time you share it. Has anyone ever challenged a number you quoted?

**Thomas:** Ask your data team for one query that already works, and read it line by line. SELECT what you want, FROM which table, WHERE these conditions hold. Changing the dates in a query somebody else wrote is a fair first step, and you learn the table names on the way.
