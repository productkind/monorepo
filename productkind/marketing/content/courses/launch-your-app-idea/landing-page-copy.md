## The learning journey

Seven stages, in the order you'll need them. Each stage is one course, and each picks up where the one before it stopped.

### 1. Plan your idea

**Course:** Plan Your Vibe Coded App: Define the Problem, User, MVP and First Prompt

You'll write down the problem your app solves, describe the person you're building it for and check that the problem is worth solving. Then you'll map what people will do in your app, cut it down to a first version you can build in one sitting and turn your plan into your first prompt. You'll finish by planning how to show your app to real people and use their feedback to decide what to change.

### 2. Build it

**Course:** Build Your First App with Lovable

You'll give Lovable your prompt and build the first version, then improve it one focused prompt at a time. You'll save data with Lovable Cloud, add Google login, connect outside services and bookmark working versions, so a mistake is easy to undo. You'll finish with your app published on a link you can share.

### 3. Learn what each part of your app does

**Course:** Basics of Software for Vibe Coding

You'll learn what the frontend, backend and database each do, and the design words for describing how your app should look. So you'll write more precise prompts, and when something needs changing, you'll know whether to ask Lovable to change the frontend, the backend or the database.

### 4. Fix what breaks

**Course:** Fix Bugs with Confidence: Debugging Your Lovable App

You'll pick up habits that cut down how often bugs appear, like building in small prompts and testing after every change, then investigate the bugs you still get the way developers do: reproduce the bug, read the browser console and Lovable's error messages, and find the cause before you ask for a fix. You'll also learn what to do when each fix Lovable makes breaks something else, and what Lovable's security warnings mean.

### 5. Change it yourself

**Course:** Save Lovable Credits: Edit Your App Like a Developer

You'll connect your app to GitHub, set up VS Code on your computer and run your app there. You'll make small changes yourself, like new wording or a different icon, hand bigger ones to GitHub Copilot, an AI agent, and publish them to your live app. None of it spends Lovable credits.

### 6. Launch it

**Course:** How to Publish a Lovable App: Custom Domain, Legal Pages, SEO and Analytics

You'll switch your app to real API keys, connect your own domain and add a privacy policy and terms of service, so people know how you'll handle their data before they sign up. You'll set up SEO so Google and AI search tools can find your app, and analytics so you can see how people use it.

### 7. Find your first customers

**Course:** Build a Business with Your Vibe Coded App: Validate Your Offer, Set a Price and Find Customers

You'll describe what your app does for people in one sentence and test it on real people. You'll set up email sign-ups, bring in your first visitors through directories and community posts, and ask your first users what to improve. Then you'll choose a price, work out how many customers you need to cover your costs and set up payments.

## How the learning journey works

- **Short challenges, at your own pace.** There are 47 challenges across the seven courses, about ten hours in total. Most take 10 to 15 minutes. The planning challenges take longer, 15 to 50 minutes, because you're writing your own plan.

- **Your own app.** You apply the steps to your own idea, so you finish with your app rather than a copy of an example.
- **Free to start.** The first challenge of every course is free. The full journey is a one-off payment of €299, with a year's access.

## Is this learning journey for you?

### Yes, if

- You have an idea for an app, or a first version you've started in Lovable.
- You don't have a technical background, and you want to build your app yourself with AI.
- You want your app live on your own domain with people using it, rather than only running in Lovable's preview.
- You want to find out whether people will pay for it.

### Not really, if

- You want to publish an app in the App Store or Google Play. This journey covers apps that open in a web browser, on a phone or a computer.
- You want to train as a software engineer. You'll change small parts of your app's code, but you won't learn a programming language.
- You need someone to certify that your app is secure or compliant. A course can't replace a professional review of your app.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Lovable?

Lovable is an AI app builder. You describe the app you want in a chat, and it builds it, including the screens, the database and the login. It also publishes your app, so other people can use it. Lovable has a free plan to start with. Building uses credits, and paid plans give you more of them.

### Do I need to know how to code?

No. You build your app by describing it to Lovable. In the Save Lovable Credits course, you'll open your app's code to change some wording or swap an icon, and the course shows you each step. You'll hand bigger changes to an AI agent. You won't learn a programming language.

### How do I know if my app idea is worth building?

That's the first thing the journey helps you check. In the planning course, you'll write down the problem your app solves and who has it, and judge whether it's worth solving before you build anything. In the last course, you'll test a one-sentence description of your app on real people and see how they respond.

### Is vibe coding bad?

It depends on how you do it. A lot of the criticism is about apps that go live without anyone testing them or checking their security. In this journey, you'll test after each change, investigate a bug before you ask for a fix, read Lovable's security warnings and keep working versions you can go back to.

### How much does it cost to make an app?

With Lovable, your main costs are a Lovable plan, a domain name once you want your own address, and this journey. In the Save Lovable Credits course, you'll make small changes without spending credits. In the last course, you'll work out how many paying customers you need to cover what your app costs to run.

### Can I use a different AI app builder?

The journey uses Lovable throughout, so the steps are written for Lovable. The planning, software basics and business courses work with any tool. The Save Lovable Credits course also works with Replit, v0 and Bolt, because they all connect to GitHub.

### Will my app be secure?

No course can promise that for every app, because the data you collect and the services you connect change the risks. You'll learn what Lovable's security warnings mean and how to fix them. Whether a warning needs action straight away depends on whether your app handles sensitive data, and you'll learn how to decide. If your app holds health, financial or other sensitive information, get a professional review too.
