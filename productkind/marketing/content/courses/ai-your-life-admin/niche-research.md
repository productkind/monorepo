## The recommendation

**Keep `mental load` in the course title. Build content around `mental load list`. Treat ADHD as a second audience, not a replacement. Publish to Google, not to video search.**

`mental load` is the largest term measured anywhere in this study and it is still accelerating. `brain dump` is roughly half its size in GB and flat. The seam worth owning is **`mental load list`**, the one query rising in GB over the last twelve months, and the course already produces exactly that: a categorised checklist you tick off.

No repositioning is needed to serve it. What needs to change is what we publish around the course.

## Vocabulary: what people actually type

Counts are YouTube autocomplete suggestions. Zero means nobody types the phrase. Ten is the ceiling, so read it as "at least ten".

**Ceiling clusters:** `mum hacks` · `mom hacks` · `mom organization` · `overwhelmed mom` · `mom burnout` · `how to get organised` · `how i organise my life` · `organise my life` · `get your life together` · `household management` · `notion family` · `sunday reset` · `adhd mom` · `how to stop feeling overwhelmed` · `mental load` · `iphone shortcuts` · `apple intelligence shortcuts` · `shortcuts ios 26` · `baby tracker`

**Real and specific:** `notion for busy moms` · `brain dump mom` · `to do list for working moms` · `adhd mum hacks` · `adhd mom organization` · `adhd mom burnout` · `how to get organised with adhd` · `apple shortcuts adhd` · `iphone shortcuts adhd` · `mental load list` · `how to share the mental load` · `explaining mental load to husband` · `chatgpt shortcut iphone` · `back tap shortcut iphone`

**Dead (zero suggestions), and this list is nearly a description of the course as we named it:** `iphone shortcuts for mums` · `iphone shortcuts for moms` · `apple shortcuts for parents` · `shortcuts for new parents` · `ai for busy mums` · `chatgpt for mums` · `mum life admin` · `new mum organisation` · `mental load mum` · `mental load app` · `baby log iphone` · `apple notes baby tracker` · `track baby feeds` · `voice to do list` · `dictate to notes iphone` · `apple notes checklist shortcut` · `apple intelligence use model` · `shortcuts use model`

Mum-shaped *AI* phrasing is dead. Mum *vocabulary* is enormous. Use theirs.

### Two keywords to avoid

- **`life admin` is contaminated.** Alphabet expansion returned Roblox admin-abuse content on 14 of 20 letters ("prison life admin script", "lana's life admin abuse"). Clean variants only: `life admin day`, `life admin system`, `life admin planner`, `notion life admin`. Fine inside a longer title, never as the bare root.
- **`apple intelligence use model` and `shortcuts use model` return nothing.** The action the course is built around has no demand under its own name. Teach it, don't title with it.

Exact matches run from 3 to 3,595 views, the page is four to ten years old, and **every single result is paper**. Not one uses voice or AI. The highest-intent video on the page is "Why Your Brain Dumps Aren't Effective", which is precisely what our shortcut fixes: paper empties your head but leaves the pile unsorted.

### `mental load`, and what the searcher is actually offered

On `how to share the mental load`, all twelve results are therapists and relationship coaches. None offers a tool.

But on `how to reduce mental load` the two biggest results are practical, and they beat the therapy videos by ten to a hundred times:

| Title | Channel | Views | Age |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 10 Game-Changing Tips for Managing the Mental Load **with ADHD** | How to ADHD | 264,923 | 1 yr |
| How to Stop Feeling Overwhelmed by Everyday Life \| Manage "Life Admin" & Reduce the Mental Load | Michela Allocca | 156,939 | **6 mo** |
| The Mental Load Is Ruining Your Relationship. Here's the Fix. | Paired (therapist) | 2,660 | 9 mo |

**Demand for practical relief is strong. What is missing is a tool.** Both winners are advice videos; neither hands you something to build. Note also that the biggest video on the mental load solution query is an ADHD one.

### `apple shortcuts adhd`, the secondary audience

Top exact match 5,865 views from a small channel, second 1,085, and two **five-year-old** videos from a sub-1,000-view channel ranking top five, in a category where the tool changed completely. Small channels rank, so authority is not the barrier, and nobody owns it. Worth cheap pages; not worth leading with, since Trends puts the whole cluster far below `mental load`.

### Notion is the competitor, and the wedge

Every completion of `how i organise my life` and `organise my life` is Notion. But mum-specific Notion content is tiny (3,751 · 2,736 · 2,178 · 1,908 views) while general Notion is enormous (4.1M · 840K · 551K). Mums are being handed a heavy system built for someone else. One shortcut you talk to is the opposite of a Notion dashboard.

### Rejected outright

- **`baby tracker app share with partner`:** stale app reviews, and the demand is explicitly for apps. Our DIY answer competes with free products that do it better, and the commercial intent points at other people's apps.
- **`apple intelligence shortcuts`:** strong demand, no gap. Stephen Robles holds four of the top twelve including 188,062 views, all recent and competent. Competing there means competing on execution with full-time tech creators.
- **`mum brain forgetting things`:** no demand, and the results are entirely medical.

## Two method notes worth keeping

**Autocomplete and supply audits describe a query's shape. Neither measures its size.** Both agreed on a rename that Trends reversed, because the proposed term turned out to be half the size of the existing one and flat. Run Trends before any naming or positioning decision, and always put a known yardstick term in the comparison.

**A huge rising percentage off a near-zero base is still near zero.** `adhd brain dump template` showed +63,550% in GB and +132,100% in US, which read as the find of the study. Sized against `mental load` in the same comparison, its mean is **0.2**. Check the mean before believing the rise.
