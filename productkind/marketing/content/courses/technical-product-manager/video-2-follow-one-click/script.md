# You only know the screens

**Runs:** about 40 seconds. **Search phrase:** "how does a web application
work". **Need it comes from:** understanding how the parts of your product fit
together.

**Treatment:** narration over a screen recording of one flow in the Little
Parrot app, then five boxes drawn on screen.

| Beat | Narration | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-6s) | "'So how does your product actually work?' You can demo every screen, and that's where it stops." | Text card, then a product screen sliding in | **You know the screens** |
| The moment (6-9s) | "You couldn't say what happens after someone taps save." | Screen recording: tapping save, then everything freezes | What happens after you tap save? |
| Why it costs you (9-14s) | "And the words come up in every design review anyway. Frontend. Backend. Database. API." | Each word dropping in on its own cut | frontend · backend · database · API |
| The shift (14-19s) | "It's five parts, and you can draw them for your own product in an afternoon." | Five boxes drawn one at a time | Five parts. One page. |
| The transformation (19-25s) | "After that, someone asks for one new field, and you can see the three parts it touches." | The drawing, three boxes lighting up one by one | One new field, three parts changed |
| The payoff (25-31s) | "So you ask which of the three is the slow one, before anybody gives you an estimate." | The question typed over the drawing | "Which of these is the slow part?" |
| Comment prompt (31-35s) | "Do you know what happens after someone taps save in your product?" | The five boxes, empty this time | Could you draw yours? |
| CTA (35-40s) | "We're building this for product managers who don't come from engineering. Link's in the comments." | Waitlist page | littleparrot.app/guides/technical-product-manager |

**Cut cadence:** nothing holds on screen for more than three seconds. The
Visual column lists every change in order, so a three-part visual is three
cuts, and the longest beats are the ones carrying the most of them.

**Channel swap:** on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, replace the last line with
"Follow for more of these" and speak the URL, then pin it in a comment. The
comment prompt stays on every channel, because it's what earns replies.

## Production notes

- **Screen recording needed:** one save or signup flow in the Little Parrot
  app, and the five boxes drawn live as the narration names them. Don't
  pre-draw the diagram, the drawing is the visual.
- **The five parts are named on screen, not explained in the narration.** The
  definitions belong in the course, not here.
