# "It's done." Two weeks later, still not live

**Runs:** about 36 seconds. **Search phrase:** "staging vs production". **Need
it comes from:** understanding the route from a change to users, the need with
the strongest evidence across communities.

**Treatment:** narration over a screen recording that follows one change to the
live Little Parrot site.

| Beat | Narration | Visual | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-4s) | "'It's done.' Two weeks later, your stakeholder still can't see it." | Text card, then a message: "so where is it?" | **"It's done." Still not live.** |
| The moment (4-9s) | "Done meant something different to everyone in that stand-up, and you couldn't tell which one." | Four people's version of "done" stacking up, one per cut | Whose "done"? |
| The shift (9-16s) | "A change sits in six places before customers get it, and only the last one is your users." | Six boxes appearing one per cut, the last one labelled with real users | Six places. Only one is live. |
| The move (16-18s) | "So you stop asking whether it's done." | The words "is it done?" crossed out | Stop asking "is it done?" |
| The transformation (18-25s) | "You ask where it is and what's left, and you get a specific answer, because that's a question engineers can answer exactly." | The new question typed, then an answer appearing against one of the six boxes | "Where is it, and what's left?" |
| The payoff (25-29s) | "And you stop promising dates you have to walk back." | A date on a roadmap, held rather than moved | No more dates you walk back |
| Comment prompt (29-31s) | "What does done mean on your team?" | The four versions of "done" again | What does "done" mean on yours? |
| CTA (31-36s) | "We're building this for product managers who don't come from engineering. Link's in the comments." | Waitlist page | littleparrot.app/guides/technical-product-manager |

**Cut cadence:** nothing holds on screen for more than three seconds. The
Visual column lists every change in order, so a three-part visual is three
cuts, and the longest beats are the ones carrying the most of them.

**Channel swap:** on TikTok, Reels and Shorts, replace the last line with
"Follow for more of these" and speak the URL, then pin it in a comment. The
comment prompt stays on every channel, because it's what earns replies.

## Production notes

- **The six places are shown, not defined.** Label the boxes on screen and let
  the viewer read them. Naming and explaining all six is what made the earlier
  version of this script lose people, and the definitions belong in the course.
- **Screen recording needed:** one real change in the Little Parrot repository
  through to the live site. A short, fast sequence, not a walkthrough.
