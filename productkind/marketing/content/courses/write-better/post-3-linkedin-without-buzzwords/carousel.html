So, name your voice. Part two of the prompt says how it should sound.

YOUR PROMPT 
Write a short LinkedIn post announcing that I've just finished a practical micro-course on AI prompting.
Tone and style:
- Friendly, approachable, understated, sincere
- Simple wording, no exaggeration
- 5 to 6 sentences, no emoji pileups
- Avoid LinkedIn buzzwords like "game-changer" or "10x productivity"
