# Structured data plan for littleparrot.app

Plan only. Nothing on the site has been changed. Investigated on 29 September 2026 from the source code in this project and the live pages at https://littleparrot.app, fetched without running JavaScript.

## Summary

The site already has structured data (hidden labels that tell search engines and AI tools what each page is about) on most public pages: an Organization (your company) block on every page, Course (a course you teach) on each course page, Article (a written guide) on each guide, Product with Offer (something for sale, with its price) on the pricing page, and FAQPage (a list of questions and answers) on several pages. The main problems are facts that do not match what visitors see. The markup says both Kinga and Tamas founded Little Parrot, while the About page says Kinga founded productkind and Tamas is an educator there. Course pages claim a €24 price and name authors, but neither appears on those pages, which currently say "Free". The home page FAQ answers are shorter than the ones on the page, and productkind's social profiles are listed as Little Parrot's own. I recommend fixing these facts and connecting everything through one shared description of Little Parrot, its website (WebSite, the site as a whole) and the two of you (Person, a named individual). I also recommend removing the FAQ markup, because Google stopped showing FAQ results in May 2026. Your pages are built into finished HTML before they are published, so Google and AI crawlers that do not run JavaScript can both already read the markup, and the new markup will reach them the same way.

## Questions for you

1. **Who founded what?** The markup says Kinga and Tamas both founded Little Parrot. Your About page says "Kinga Magyar is the founder of productkind" and "Tamas Kokeny is an educator at productkind". _Recommended answer:_ follow the About page. Mark Kinga as founder of productkind and Tamas as someone who works at productkind, with no founder claim for Little Parrot itself. If Tamas is a co-founder, change the About page wording first and the markup will follow it.
2. **Which social profiles belong to Little Parrot, and which to productkind?** Today Little Parrot's markup lists productkind's Instagram, YouTube and Substack, plus a LinkedIn product page (linkedin.com/products/productkind-littleparrotapp) that is not linked anywhere on the site. _Recommended answer:_ Little Parrot gets only TikTok (@littleparrot.app, linked in the footer). productkind gets LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube and Substack. Leave out the LinkedIn product page unless you add a visible link to it.
3. **Which logo should search engines use?** The markup points to the wide `logo-little-parrot.svg`. _Recommended answer:_ use `https://littleparrot.app/logo-square.png` (the 1080 x 1080 parrot on the gradient). Google asks for a logo at least 112 x 112 pixels that looks right on a white background, and a square image is the safest choice.
4. **Remove the FAQ markup?** It is on the home page, the two waitlist pages and most guides. Google stopped showing FAQ results on 7 May 2026, and the questions are already readable in the page HTML. _Recommended answer:_ yes, remove it everywhere. If you prefer to keep it for AI tools, the answers must be copied word for word from the page, which the home page currently does not do.
5. **Should the two waitlist pages still be marked as courses?** `/guides/technical-product-manager` and `/guides/building-apps-with-ai` are marked as Course, but the pages say the learning paths are "still being built" and "we are deciding whether to build this". _Recommended answer:_ mark them as ordinary web pages until the paths open, then switch them to Course.
6. **Should course pages show who wrote them?** The course markup names Kinga and Tamas as authors, but no names appear on course pages. _Recommended answer:_ add a short visible line such as "Written by Kinga Magyar and Tamas Kokeny" to each course overview page (a visible content change that needs your approval), then keep author in the markup. Until that line exists, remove author from course markup. Please also confirm both of you wrote every course (llms.txt says so).
7. **Breadcrumb trail on course pages?** Course pages are marked with a "Home > Courses > course" trail that visitors do not see. Guides and collection pages do show one. _Recommended answer:_ remove the trail from course pages for now. Add it back if you add a visible breadcrumb to them.

## What will change

1. Every public page: one shared, corrected description of Little Parrot, productkind, the website, Kinga and Tamas, each with a fixed identifier (an @id) so all pages point to the same things.
2. Course overview pages (9 courses): Course markup that uses only what the page shows. The price, audience, level, generated description and (unless question 6 is accepted) the authors come out.
3. Courses page: a clean list of the courses in the form Google's course list result expects.
4. Guide articles (10): Article markup linked to the shared author and publisher, with FAQ markup removed.
5. Pricing page: the membership and its two prices, linked to Little Parrot.
6. Home page: WebSite markup (helps Google show "Little Parrot" as the site name), with FAQ markup removed.
7. About page: marked as the About page, with Kinga and Tamas described from their bios.
8. Guides index and the four audience collections: collection pages that point to the same course and article identifiers.
9. The two waitlist pages: changed from Course to plain web pages, with FAQ markup removed.

## Implementation details

### Findings from the investigation

**How pages are delivered (checked).** The site is a Vite + React single-page app, but `npm run build` also runs `vite build --ssr src/entry-server.tsx` and `scripts/prerender.mjs`. The prerender script renders each public route with `renderToString` and writes a static `index.html` per route. It puts react-helmet-async's `<title>`, meta, link and script tags into `<head>`. Everything the `SEO` component (`src/components/SEO.tsx`) outputs, including `<script type="application/ld+json">`, is therefore in the HTML the server sends. I confirmed this on the live site with `curl` using a GPTBot user agent (no JavaScript): `/`, `/courses`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/guides`, a hub, an article, a waitlist page and a course page all return their JSON-LD in the first response. So Google and AI crawlers that do not run JavaScript both see it. No change to the delivery method is needed. New markup must go through the same `SEO` component so the prerender picks it up.

Caveats found:

- **Data is fixed at publish time.** Course data comes from Supabase when the site is built. Changing a course in the database does not update its markup until the site is republished. The same applies to the visible "Free until 18 October" badge, which is also baked into the static HTML.
- **Non-prerendered URLs serve the home page.** `/auth`, `/nest/*`, `/promo`, `/:id/challenge`, `/certificate/:id` and unknown URLs all return HTTP 200 with the prerendered home page HTML: home title, `canonical` = `/`, `index, follow`, and the home page JSON-LD. The correct `noindex` only appears after JavaScript runs. Google renders JavaScript and will see `noindex`. Crawlers that do not run JavaScript see a copy of the home page. This is a hosting and routing issue, not a structured data one. It is flagged here and is not part of this plan.
- `public/sitemap.xml` (checked in) still lists `/promo` and is stale. The build writes a fresh `dist/sitemap.xml`, which is the one served live and is correct. It does not affect structured data.

### Where the markup lives and how it reaches the HTML

- New module `src/lib/structuredData.ts`: `SITE_URL`, the `@id` constants, builders for the site-wide nodes (`siteGraphNodes()`), and helpers such as `courseId(url)`, `articleId(slug)` and `webPageNode({...})`. Person data comes from `src/content/guides/authors.ts`, extended with the About page description if needed, so bylines and markup share one source.
- `src/components/SEO.tsx`: keep the `jsonLd` prop, but emit one `<script type="application/ld+json">` holding `{"@context":"https://schema.org","@graph":[...siteGraphNodes(), ...pageNodes]}`. Skip the script entirely when `noIndex` is true.
- Remove the static JSON-LD block from `index.html`. Otherwise it duplicates the site-wide nodes on every page, and the prerender script does not strip it.
- Update the page builders in `src/pages/Index.tsx`, `About.tsx`, `Courses.tsx`, `CourseOverview.tsx`, `Pricing.tsx`, `Guides.tsx`, `Guide.tsx` (hub and article), `TechnicalProductManagerWaitlist.tsx` and `BuildingAppsWithAiWaitlist.tsx` to pass page nodes that use the shared ids. Remove the `faqSchema` usage. Leave `TechnicalProductConfidence.tsx` alone or align it the same way, since it is not currently served.
- Delivery stays as it is: Helmet → `src/entry-server.tsx` (`helmet.script.toString()`) → `scripts/prerender.mjs` writes it into each route's static HTML. No change to the prerender script is needed. After the change, publish the site so the build and prerender run.

### Implementation rules

1. Every value must come from the same variable or constant that renders the visible text on that page. Never type facts into the markup by hand when the page renders them from data.
2. Do not add prices, ratings, reviews, testimonials, learner counts, company logos ("previously at"), awards, credentials, addresses, phone numbers or dates that the page does not show.
3. Do not add `offers`, `isAccessibleForFree` or any "free until" wording to Course markup. Prices live only on `/pricing`.
4. Use the absolute canonical URL (the same string as the page's `<link rel="canonical">`) for `url` and for building `@id`s. Course URLs keep the UUID form.
5. One JSON-LD script per page, with `@graph`. No inline duplicate `Organization` or `Person` objects: always refer by `@id`.
6. Omit a property rather than emit it empty, `undefined` or invalid (for example no `timeRequired` "PT", no empty `teaches`).
7. Strip markdown from text values (`stripMarkdown`), and escape `<` as `<` in the serialised JSON so content can never close the script tag.
8. No structured data on `noIndex` pages.
9. Question answers change the rules: until the owner confirms question 6, omit Course `author`. Until question 7 is answered, omit the course breadcrumb.
10. Do not change visible copy as part of this work, except where the owner approves it (question 6).

### Checks after implementing

1. Build in a scratch copy or CI (not in the owner's working folder, since the build writes `dist/`), then for each template's example URLs check that `dist/<route>/index.html` contains exactly one `application/ld+json` script and that it parses as JSON.
2. After publishing, fetch live pages without JavaScript (`curl -A "GPTBot" https://littleparrot.app/...`) and confirm the markup is in the server HTML for `/`, `/about`, `/courses`, `/pricing`, `/guides`, one hub, one article by each author, both waitlist pages and three course pages.
3. Schema.org Validator (https://validator.schema.org/) on the same URLs: no errors, and all `@id` references resolve within the page's graph.
4. Google Rich Results Test (https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) on `/courses` and a course page (Course list), an article (Article, Breadcrumb), a hub (Breadcrumb) and `/pricing` (Product snippet). Record any warnings.
5. Compare the markup with the visible page: every name, date, price, author and list item in the JSON-LD can be found on the page. Check especially that course descriptions match the page and that no course carries a price.
6. Confirm the same `#organization`, `#website` and Person nodes are byte-for-byte identical across pages.
7. Confirm `noIndex` pages (for example the prerendered HTML for `/auth` after JavaScript runs) carry no JSON-LD.
8. After a few weeks, check Search Console → Enhancements for Course list, Breadcrumbs, Product snippets and Article.

### Google documentation checked

I read Google's current documentation on 29 September 2026: the Search gallery of supported features, and the Course list, Article, Breadcrumb, Organization, Product snippet, Site names and general structured data guidelines pages (most last updated 8 September 2026). I also read the Search Central changelog, which records the FAQ rich result as no longer shown from 7 May 2026. Judgements based on those pages are verified as of that date. Two points remain unverified: whether Google will show a price snippet or merchant listing for a subscription membership, and how Google handles Course list eligibility when courses sit behind a subscription. Google's rules change often, so re-check them before relying on any rich result.

### Flags: duplicated, contradictory, unnecessary or uncertain

- **Duplicated:** two unconnected Little Parrot `Organization`s per page (shell + inline `provider`/`publisher`/`brand`), and Person objects repeated inline on every course and article.
- **Contradictory:** the founder claim contradicts About. Course `offers` €24 contradicts the visible "Free". productkind profiles are listed as Little Parrot's `sameAs`. The home FAQ answer text differs from the visible text. Hub `name` uses the meta title, not the visible H1. Hub breadcrumb label "Guides" differs from the visible "All guides".
- **Unnecessary:** FAQPage everywhere (no Google feature). Course `audience`, `educationalLevel`, `about`. Hub `isRelatedTo`. The `provider.sameAs` pointing to the site itself.
- **Uncertain:** Product rich results for a subscription. `Course` for waitlist paths (question 5). Authors on course pages (question 6). Markup freshness depends on republishing after database edits. Non-prerendered URLs serve the home page's HTML and markup to crawlers that do not run JavaScript (a hosting issue, outside this plan).
