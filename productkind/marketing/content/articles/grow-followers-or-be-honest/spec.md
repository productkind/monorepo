# Carousel: Would you rather grow your follower count or be honest?

**Source article:** `thoughts/assets/articles/23-would-you-rather-grow-your-follower-count-or-be-honest.md`
**Brand:** productkind (Thoughts by productkind article)
**Channels:** LinkedIn (PDF document post), Instagram, TikTok (PNGs), Threads (PNGs, productkind profile)
**Date:** July 2026 (article scheduled 2026-07-28)

**One takeaway:** honest accounts of building a business are more helpful
to read than the polished highlight reel, and the reason the shiny version
is so tempting is the constant comparison we can't switch off.

**Story arc:** the honest dilemma that opens the piece, project success or
be honest (slide 1), the two paths laid out plainly (2), the honest
admission that on paper it looks fine but often doesn't feel it (3), why
it's so hard, naming the constant comparison (4), and how it never ends,
even for billionaires, source named (5), the turn, closing the apps and
asking why I'm doing this at all (6),
the realisation that an honest picture helps more than the façade (7), a
beat that turns the real question on the reader (8), the invitation to the
full real-time article, with a share-ask (9). A reflection piece: the
throughline is the honest telling itself, with a genuine question as the
payoff, no bolted-on how-to.
