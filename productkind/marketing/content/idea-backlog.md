### "How I use AI for [life thing]" episode areas

Chosen for recurrence in the audience's life, a visible before/after, and an iPhone/Apple Intelligence tie-in where possible. Each episode ends with the bridge: the same skill (specificity, context, small steps) is how you build an app later.

1. School and family logistics: photo of the school letter, out come the dates, the calendar entries, the packing list. The mental-load capture.
2. Meal planning and the shopping list (the use she already has; show the upgrade from "give me recipes" to a specific, constrained prompt).
3. Deciphering official paperwork: insurance letters, contracts, bills, and drafting the appeal or complaint reply.
4. Doctor's appointment prep: turn worries into a question list, summarise a letter to understand it (prepare, never diagnose).
5. The subscription audit and comparing offers: money admin with a number at the end.
6. Travel planning under real constraints (toddler, budget, three free hours).
7. Wardrobe and colour analysis (the validated engagement winner in this niche).
8. Kids' occasions: birthday party logistics, gift shortlists.
9. iPhone-native wins: summarised voice notes, priority notifications, photo cleanup (ties to the life-admin course).
10. Career admin: the CV refresh and LinkedIn About rewrite (bridge for the career-changer segment).
