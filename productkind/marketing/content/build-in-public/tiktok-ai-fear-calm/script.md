# The fear one — script

Bucket: myth-bust / calm view (the channel's anchor theme, tackling the fear
around AI). Source: `../ai-panic-video-linkedin.md`. Written in
personal-tone-of-voice. Post early; it carries the intro's fear thread.

**Treatment:** Kinga to camera, calm, roughly 48 seconds. No screen recording
needed (optional scary-headline B-roll behind the hook). Note: the source post
opens "You are not falling behind. The panic is being sold to you," which is a
negation-then-reversal we avoid, so this opens on a real moment instead.

| Beat | Spoken (to camera) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-4s) | "Every time I open LinkedIn, someone's warning me I'm falling behind on AI. And we teach this stuff." | Falling behind on AI? |
| The turn (4-15s) | "A lot of the women who join our courses say the same. They arrive feeling like everyone else got a head start. So I looked into where all that urgency actually comes from." | — |
| The why (15-30s) | "A lot of it comes from venture-backed AI companies that need huge growth to meet their investors' expectations. If you're scared of being left behind, you start paying sooner. The worry has a business model behind it." | — |
| The payoff (30-42s) | "So now, when a headline makes me anxious, I ask one question. What does the person who wrote this gain from me feeling this way? It usually calms me right down." | — |
| CTA (42-48s) | "You can start with AI at your own pace. Follow along if you'd like to learn about it calmly." | littleparrot.app |

**Payoff/technique:** the one question to ask an anxiety-inducing headline
("what does its author gain from me feeling this way?").
