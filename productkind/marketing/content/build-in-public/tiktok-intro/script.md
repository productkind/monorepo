# Intro TikTok — script

The pinned intro video for the Kinga-fronted TikTok experiment (see
`../../../channels/tiktok/experiment-brief.md`). Built from
`thoughts/assets/articles/020-my-journey-from-employee-to-running-a-business.md`,
written in personal-tone-of-voice.

**Treatment:** Kinga to camera throughout, roughly 52 seconds, no screen
recording. Tamas is named but does not appear on camera. Warm and plain, not a
pitch. Pin it to the top of the profile so it is the first thing a new visitor
taps. On-screen captions mirror the spoken words (most people watch on mute
first).

| Beat | Spoken (to camera) | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-4s) | "Only 14% of the people building with AI are women. I'd really like to help change that." | **Only 14% of people building with AI are women.** |
| Who + camera honesty (4-14s) | "Hi, I'm Kinga. And honestly, talking into a camera really isn't my natural habitat. I spent eight years as a product manager in tech, and at the end of 2024 I left my corporate job to build things that actually help people." | Kinga · ex-PM, 8 years |
| What (14-27s) | "So now it's me and my partner Tamas, a two-person company called productkind. We build Little Parrot, where we help women build things that felt impossible before AI, even if they've never written a line of code. That might be a whole app, or just automating the part of your work you dread." | productkind · a 2-person company |
| The fear (27-38s) | "There's a lot of fear around AI right now. That you're behind, that it's coming for your job. I'd rather show you how to actually use it than add to the worry." | — |
| The channel + honest bit (38-52s) | "So here I'll share honestly how we run a tiny business with AI. The real prompts, what works, and to be honest, a lot of our numbers that aren't glamorous yet. But I'm happy to share what doesn't work too, because someone out there can learn from it. So follow along if you're building something, or you'd like to." | Free challenges & toolkits → **littleparrot.app** |

**Spoken search phrase** (for TikTok's search index): "building with AI" and
"Little Parrot" both land naturally in the script.

**Follow CTA** is payoff-anchored (honest behind-the-scenes of building a
company with AI, including the failures) and placed at the end, never as the
hook. The destination littleparrot.app is spoken and shown on screen (no
clickable TikTok bio link until 1,000 followers; the URL is short enough to
type).

**Positioning note:** the headline promise is helping women build what felt
impossible before AI. Automating the work you don't enjoy is one example, and
the fear reframe (rather than the source article's "made by tech bros" framing)
is a supporting theme.

Captions for TikTok and Instagram Reels are in `captions.md`.
