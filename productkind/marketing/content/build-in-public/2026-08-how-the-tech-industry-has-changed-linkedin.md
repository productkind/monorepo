# How has the tech industry changed in the past year and a half

**Angle:** AI tremendously influenced the tech industry, and people get disillusioned. I saw a reel that made me think how it has changed for people to work in the tech industry.
**Instagram reel:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbeWEnDOVIB/?igsh=cjZic2oxNjE5aWJn

---

I saw a reel yesterday where a software engineer argued that the tech industry has become less enjoyable for two reasons:

1. Coding used to be about figuring out how to solve difficult problems. Now, much of the work is telling AI what to build and asking it to fix things when they break.

2. Great ideas and creative designs can be copied so quickly that success increasingly comes down to marketing.

I’ve been thinking about whether I agree.

I still think there's a lot of creativity in building software. But AI has definitely taken over some of the creative tasks (like creating UI designs and user flows).

What I do agree with is that the pressure to produce more, and produce it faster, has definitely increased.

What do you see? How has the tech industry changed for you over the past year and a half?
