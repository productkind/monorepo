# Channel registry

The places Little Parrot posts into, and the rules for each one. The promo-fanout skill reads this file to decide which drafts to write and how to shape them.

**How to use:** keep this file current. When you join a new community or learn a community's posting rules, add or update its block here once. The more accurate this is, the less editing you do on each draft.

**Utm params:** most channels add `?utm_source=`, `&utm_medium=`, `&utm_campaign=` and `&utm_content=` parameters to links so you can see in analytics which channel drove a click.

---

## LinkedIn (productkind)

- **Type:** public social, professional
- **Audience:** mixed (peers, partners, potential learners). Non-technical women learning AI-assisted development are the people we most want to reach.
- **Format:** 90 to 200 words. Hook, insight, implication. No hashtags. Whitespace between paragraphs. End on a reframe or question, not a summary. (Use the `linkedin-post` skill.)
- **Self-promo rules:** fine to post, but lead with value the reader gets, not the product. One sentence of Little Parrot context is enough.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=pk&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]&utm_content=[topic-slug]`
- **Posting from:** Company page (productkind): https://www.linkedin.com/company/productkind/ - "we" voice.
- **Assets:** can attach a cover image or video.

## Substack Notes

- **Type:** newsletter platform. Notes is posted from Kinga's personal account.
- **Audience:** existing readers and Substack's network. Warmer, already curious.
- **Format:** Notes are reflective and can explore nuance and uncertainty. No jargon or hype. (Use the `linkedin-post` skill Substack Notes section.)
- **Self-promo rules:** your own channel, so promotion is expected, but keep it generous and thinking-led, and provide value to the reader.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=substack&utm_medium=note&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]&utm_content=[topic-slug]`
- **Posting from:** Notes from the profile: https://substack.com/@kingamagyar
- **Assets:** optional image

## Instagram (productkind company account)

- **Type:** visual-first public social
- **Account:** https://www.instagram.com/by_productkind/
- **Audience:** broad; women interested in building, founders, the productkind community.
- **Format:** the visual is the post (image, reel or carousel). "We" voice (company account), warm. Caption: engaging hook, a little context, then the call to action. Links are NOT clickable in captions, so the CTA is "link in bio" (or a Story with a link sticker). Five relevant hashtags should be used.
- **Self-promo rules:** own account, promotion expected; keep it warm and mission and value-led.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]&utm_content=bio` (put it on the bio link; caption links aren't clickable, so they can't carry a tag inline).
- **Assets:** image, reel or carousel.

## Slack communities

## Geek Girls email list

- **Type:** community email list. Geek Girls is a Portuguese community for women in tech.
- **Audience:** women in tech in Portugal.
- **Format:** a simple, plain-text email you send by hand. No HTML template. Subject line plus a short, warm body, written like a personal note. One masked link.
- **Self-promo rules:** make it value-first, keep it warm and member-first.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=email&utm_medium=ggpt&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]`

## WhatsApp groups

One shared draft for all WhatsApp groups. You post it into whichever groups it suits.

- **Audience:** members of informal women-in-tech / community groups.
- **Format:** very short, conversational, no markdown headers. A single message, ideally under 60 words.
- **Self-promo rules:** sharing is welcome as long as it serves the group members' interest, so lead with what value they get, not the offer.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=[group-name]&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]`

## Reddit

One shared draft. You choose the subreddits and post where it fits the rules.

> Reddit is the strictest channel. Most subreddits enforce a self-promotion ratio(roughly 1 promotional post per ~9 normal contributions). Posts that read as ads get removed and can get the account banned.

- **Audience:** women learning to build with AI, beginner-builder communities.
- **Format:** a helpful post or comment first; the offer is secondary. No marketing tone.
- **Self-promo rules:** lead with genuine value, mention the free offer as a footnote, and check the subreddit's rules before posting.
- **Utm param:** `utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=[subreddit-name]&utm_campaign=[campaign-slug]&utm_content=[post-name]`

## Shared copy rules (apply to every channel)

- Use the skill mentioned at the channel description.
- Describe experiences, not features ("you get", "you can").
- Thank partners by name (e.g. Lovable, SheBuilds organisers).
- One sentence of Little Parrot context is enough for a new audience.
- Frame around the learner's win and the deadline, not "look at us".
- Sign-offs only where the channel expects them (email). Social posts don't sign off.
