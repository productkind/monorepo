# LinkedIn About Section

Kinga's personal LinkedIn profile. Last updated 2026-08-07.

I help women build what felt impossible before AI.

I'm the founder of productkind, a small education company, and most of my time right now goes into LittleParrot.app, a science-based microlearning platform that teaches women to build with AI. When Lovable published its numbers in 2026, only 14% of its users were women. These tools are simple enough for anyone to use, but most of the material that teaches them assumes you already know how software gets built. That's what I'm trying to change.

I've spent a decade in tech as a product leader, mostly working out which problems are worth solving and how to get them built. So these days I write, teach and build in the open. In my newsletter, Thoughts by productkind, I share honest accounts of running a small business: the experiments that worked, the ones that flopped, and what each one taught us. To be honest, the flops are usually the more useful ones to write about.

I also organise ProductTank Lisbon, and getting product people in a room together is still one of my favourite things to do.

If you've ever thought building your own product wasn't for you, come and follow along. I'd love to have you.
