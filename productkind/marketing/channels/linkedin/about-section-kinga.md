## About

As coding agents get more capable, I think product managers and founders need more technical understanding, not less: to weigh trade-offs, discuss implementation with engineers and guide AI agents more effectively.

I’m the founder of Little Parrot. In our hands-on technical courses, product managers and founders work in a real app that trains them to make better technical decisions. So the next time a conversation with engineers, or an AI agent, gets technical, they know what to ask next.

I’ve worked in tech for 10 years, 8 of them building software products as a product manager. After a coding bootcamp, I worked as a software engineer before moving into product. Since then I’ve led product teams on ecommerce, sales platforms and enterprise automation, most recently as lead product manager at Volkswagen Digital Solutions, across a cluster of four product teams.

I’m also a certified executive coach and have coached product managers through new roles and promotions. And I co-organise ProductTank Lisbon, bringing the local product community together to learn from each other.

I’d especially love to see more women in product with the technical confidence to shape what gets built.

I write about technical skills for product managers, building products with AI, and what we’re learning at Little Parrot. If that’s your world too, follow along or say hello.

## Experience: Chief Product Officer, Little Parrot

At Little Parrot, product managers and founders learn how software works hands-on, in a real app, through cohort-based and self-paced courses.

I lead product strategy, discovery and delivery for our courses and the littleparrot.app learning platform, including positioning, pricing and go-to-market.

More than 1,000 learners have signed up since June 2025. 88% of them started learning, and 18% of those earned a certificate.

- Product strategy and positioning: decide which courses we build, who they're for, and how they're priced and sold.
- User research: interview product managers and founders, and run willingness-to-pay, search-demand and community research, so course and pricing decisions rest on evidence.
- Course design: create the curriculum and missions of our courses, and the free workshops that lead to them.
- Building the product: build littleparrot.app with AI agents together with Tamas, from course pages and checkout to onboarding and a blended learning mode for live workshops.
- Learner emails and analytics: write and run the emails learners get, from welcome to course reminders, and track how learners sign up, progress and buy in PostHog and Supabase.
- Growth experiments: test positioning and channels with landing pages, LinkedIn posts and short videos, and share what we learn.
- AI workflows: build the Claude Code skills and review agents we use to turn our own course expertise into course material, emails and posts faster.

Our mission is to help women build their own products, and their own income, with AI.
