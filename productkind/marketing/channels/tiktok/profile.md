# TikTok profile copy

Profile copy for the Kinga-fronted TikTok experiment (see `experiment-brief.md`).

## Handle

@kingabuilds

Chosen because `kingamagyar` (Kinga's handle on LinkedIn and Instagram) and its
close variants were all taken on TikTok. `kingabuilds` is short, easy to say in
a video CTA, and signals the topic.

- **Tradeoff:** it diverges from the `kingamagyar` identity used elsewhere. To
  help people connect the dots, keep the real first name in the display name;
  consider "Kinga Magyar · building with AI" so anyone who knows her from
  LinkedIn or Instagram recognises her.
- **Claim `kingabuilds` on Instagram too** before the eventual cross-post, so
  the two channels match.

## Name field (separate from the @handle, and searchable)

Kinga · building with AI

Put a topic keyword here so the account surfaces in search; it is indexed
separately from the bio.

## Bio (up to 160 characters)

Helping women build what felt impossible before AI
Product leader & founder of productkind, sharing the non-glamorous parts too
LittleParrot.app 🦜

## Notes

- Comfortably under 160 characters, with room to adjust the lines.
- `littleparrot.app` is plain text, not a clickable link. TikTok only unlocks the
  clickable website field at 1,000 followers, so until then the destination also
  lives in each video's pinned comment and is spoken/shown on screen.
- Write the link without an `@`: on TikTok `@littleparrot.app` would read as a
  TikTok account tag, not the website.
- **Trigger at 1,000 followers:** add the clickable website link, and revisit
  the bio then (a link changes what the last line should say).
