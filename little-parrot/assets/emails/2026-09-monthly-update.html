What you might not know yet

You can edit your app on your own computer

Our Save Lovable Credits course has been on Little Parrot since June, and it's one of the four Lovable courses we've just updated. It shows you how to set up a workspace on your computer, which gives you more control over your app. Once it's set up, you can:

  • Make small changes yourself, like copy and icons, without spending Lovable credits
  • See every change in your app the moment you save it
  • Go back to any saved version of your app, using its version history in GitHub
  • Describe bigger changes to GitHub Copilot, an AI agent, and review its work

Built with Replit, v0 or Bolt instead? The same setup works for those too.
