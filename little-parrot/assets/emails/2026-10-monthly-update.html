01Build an App with Lovable That People Pay For

In our research with women building with AI, we kept hearing the same problem: “I can't judge what the AI is doing.” This self-paced course is our answer.

Over seven missions, you plan your own app idea, build it, launch it on your own domain and look for your first paying customers. Along the way, you learn what the frontend, backend and database do, so you can judge what Lovable is doing, and you make small changes yourself without spending credits.

It comes with lifetime access to the missions, plus six months of our private community and monthly office hours, live calls where you bring your questions to Kinga (Lead Product Manager) and Tamas (Principal Software Engineer).

The first mission is free, so you can try it before you pay.

See the Lovable course

02For product managers: a three-week course

Product managers told us a deeper technical understanding would make them more confident when they talk to developers. Technical Foundations for Product Managers is a cohort-based course. You run a real product in a development environment, explore the different layers of the software, ship and roll back a change, and answer your own data questions.

It takes three weeks, with weekly live office hours. The first cohort starts on Tuesday 19 January 2027, and your employer can pay for it. You can do the first mission live at our Lisbon workshop (below).

Know a product manager who'd like this? Please send them the course page.
