Free workshop in Lisbon

Hi {{FirstName}},

On Friday 13 November, we're running a free, hands-on workshop in Lisbon as part of Portugal Tech Week: How web apps work. You don't need any technical knowledge.

We made it after several product managers told us that a deeper technical understanding would make them more confident when they talk to developers. If you build with AI, you'll also practise giving an AI agent a bug report with real evidence, which you can do the next time something breaks in your own app.

So far, only men have signed up, none of them product managers, and we'd love more women in the room. If it could help you at work, or you're just curious, please come.

Friday 13 November, 19:00 to 21:30

At PTW Palace, Rua Gomes Freire 98, Lisbon. It's free and in English.

Save your place

Bring a laptop, ideally a personal one, because some company laptops can't open the tools we use. Before the day, we'll email you how to set up a free GitHub account. We'll have snacks too.

In the workshop, you'll:

  • Run a real app in your own development environment, which opens in your browser, so you see the parts developers talk about: the frontend, the server and the database
  • Follow a failed save (a customer clicks Save and their new record never appears) through each of those parts, so you can tell where a problem starts
  • Write a bug report with the evidence a developer needs, then give it to GitHub Copilot, an AI agent, and test its fix

Know someone in Lisbon?

If you can't come, a friend or colleague in Lisbon might, especially a woman who works in product or builds with AI. Please pass on the link to the workshop.
