Your Monday move: the network tab at work

In the course, every mission ends with a Monday move: the same skill, used on your own product at work. Here's the one for the workshop. It takes about 15 minutes, and you don't need access to the code.

  1. Open your product in your browser, then open the network tab before you click anything.
  2. Do one action, such as saving a form, and find the request it sent.
  3. Read its status and its response. A 200 can still hide an error, as Twenty's did, so look inside the response too.

If you find something that looks wrong, the bug report template in the guide is ready to copy. Read its Share evidence safely section before you paste anything into a ticket: the network tab can show your login details and your customers' data.
