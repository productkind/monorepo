Technical skills for product managers

Hi {{first_name}},

Product managers in our research described feeling like “the least knowledgeable person in the room” during technical conversations.

Knowing when to use an API request or a webhook gives you a way to join in when your team discusses how to get information from another system.

API request: get information on demand

Your product asks another system for information when it needs it. That system sends a response.

Example: A customer opens their booking. Your product asks the payment provider for the current payment status so it can show it on screen.

Webhook: react when an event happens

The other system sends your product a notification when something changes. Your product can act on the update without repeatedly checking for it.

Example: A payment succeeds. The payment provider notifies your product, which can then send the customer a booking confirmation.
