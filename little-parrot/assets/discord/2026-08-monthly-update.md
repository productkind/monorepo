### 🧠 Hand Off the Mental Load

Build two iPhone voice shortcuts in about 20 minutes. One sorts a spoken brain dump into shared checklists. The other saves feeds, nappies and naps as timestamped lines in one running note.

You'll need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer, because those have Apple's built-in AI. **The course is free for everyone until 30 August.**
