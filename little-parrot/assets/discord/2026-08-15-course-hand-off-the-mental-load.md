# New course: Hand Off the Mental Load 🧠

Hey everyone,

You talk, and your phone sorts everything on your mind into checklists your partner can tick off without being asked. That's our new course, made especially for mums, and it's **free for everyone until 30 August**.

## 🎙️ The Brain Dump

Ramble in any order. It comes back in categorised checklists, shared to your partner's phone. About 6 minutes to build.

## 📝 The Baby Log

Say a feed, a nappy or a nap, and it's saved as a timestamped line in one running note you can show your health visitor.

👉 [Build your shortcuts](https://littleparrot.app/55dfd25b-b9a5-4f04-860d-ef5c1d1e20b9/course-overview?utm_source=discord-lp&utm_campaign=mental-load-aug26)

You build both in the Shortcuts app that's already on your phone, using the AI that's built in, and you'll need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer. After 30 August the first challenge stays free and the other three become part of the subscription.
