## 🔄 Every Lovable course is up to date

Lovable shipped changes on 61 separate days between June and September, so we updated the steps and screenshots in all four of our Lovable courses. New in them:

- **Chat mode:** ask Lovable questions about your app for free, within a daily allowance. You'll find it in the [Build Your First App with Lovable](https://littleparrot.app/5e86e580-264c-442c-8cc4-be5645f13e87/course-overview?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=lp&utm_campaign=sep26) course.
- **SEO & AI search scan:** a free check that shows what stops Google and AI search tools from reading your app. You'll find it in the [How to Publish a Lovable App](https://littleparrot.app/ff736c4f-8ccc-41b6-96c6-68806804c5d8/course-overview?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=lp&utm_campaign=sep26) course.
- **Drafts:** try a risky bug fix in a separate copy of your app, and keep it only if it works. You'll find it in the [Fix Bugs with Confidence](https://littleparrot.app/aaab45b7-209a-4578-a515-3e55687f0c53/course-overview?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=lp&utm_campaign=sep26) course.
- **Lovable or your own computer?** Use Chat mode to ask whether a change has to be built in Lovable. If it doesn't, make it yourself on your own computer, without spending credits. You'll find it in the [Save Lovable Credits](https://littleparrot.app/7171137b-9461-425f-8737-ad3400d56fd8/course-overview?utm_source=discord&utm_medium=lp&utm_campaign=sep26) course.
