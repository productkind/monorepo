## Troubleshooting Your Shortcuts

This gets your Shortcuts working again when something misbehaves. It's a fix-it lookup for the two iPhone Shortcuts in this course: the **Brain Dump** (you talk, and AI sorts it into checklist notes) and the **Baby Log** (you talk, and AI saves timestamped lines to one note). Find your symptom below, tap it, and read the fix.

### The Brain Dump

<Accordion type="single" collapsible>

<AccordionItem value="invented-list">
<AccordionTrigger>You tapped it, said nothing, and it invented a random list</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

If you tap the shortcut but say nothing, the AI still tries to fill all three categories, so it makes items up. The fix is a check that stops the shortcut when you haven't said anything.

Add an **If** action, and set its condition to **Dictated Text** → **has any value**. Drag it up so it sits right below **Dictate Text**, and drag the **End If** down to the very bottom, so all your other actions sit between them. Now if you say nothing, nothing gets saved.

This is the guard from the **"skip the accidental taps"** step in the course. If your shortcut invents a list, it's usually missing, or a few actions have slipped outside it, so check every action after **Dictate Text** sits between the **If** and the **End If**.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="empty-category-stall">
<AccordionTrigger>A category pops up "What checklist item name?" and the shortcut stops</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

When a category has nothing in it, its **Append Checklist Item** action runs with nothing to save, so the shortcut stops and asks you to type a name.

Each category is built from three actions in a row: **Get Dictionary Value**, **Combine Text**, and **Append Checklist Item**. The fix is to add a check around that last one. Add an **If** action set to **Combined Text** → **has any value**, and drag the **Append Checklist Item** so it sits between the **If** and the **End If** (leave **Get Dictionary Value** and **Combine Text** above the **If**). Do this for all three categories: Logistics, Food & Household, and Misc. Now an empty category is skipped instead of stopping the shortcut.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="nothing-sorted">
<AccordionTrigger>Nothing sorted, or you saw a brief error</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Every so often the AI slips up and doesn't answer in the right format. Just run the shortcut and do the dictation again, it almost always sorts cleanly the second time.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="dictation-cut-off">
<AccordionTrigger>The dictation cut off before you finished, and nothing was saved</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Two causes. The screen may have dimmed mid-dictation, so keep it awake and give it a tap if it starts to dim. Or **Stop Listening** is set to stop on a pause: open the **Dictate Text** action (tap the blue arrow), and set **Stop Listening** to **On Tap** so it waits for your tap before finishing. That suits a rambly dump where you pause to think.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="wrong-category">
<AccordionTrigger>Items land in the wrong category</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

For example "buy raspberries" ends up in Logistics. That's the AI deciding where each item fits, and a leading verb like "buy" can pull it the wrong way. Tighten the category definitions in your prompt: list **Food & Household first** and let it own "buy, get, or restock", and add "(not shopping)" to Logistics. Or just move the odd item by hand in Notes.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="stripped-meaning">
<AccordionTrigger>An item comes back stripped of its meaning</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

For example "call my partner" loses "about dinner tonight". The prompt is over-trimming. Make sure your sorting prompt includes this line, which tells the AI to hold on to the details:

```
Trim filler words, but keep the details that make each item make sense on its own later: what it is, why, and any time or day.
```

If it's missing, add it just before the final `Here is what I said:` line.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="one-line">
<AccordionTrigger>All the items run together on one line in the note</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Scroll to your **Combine Text** action and set it to combine with **New Lines** (not Spaces), so each item sits on its own line and becomes its own checklist item.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="get-dictionary-source">
<AccordionTrigger>A category is empty or has the wrong items, even though you mentioned items for it</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

This usually means that category's **Get Dictionary Value** action is reading from the wrong place. On the second and third categories (Food & Household and Misc), **Get Dictionary Value** often points its **Dictionary** field at the action just above it, instead of at the AI's answer, so it can't find that category's items. Tap that field and set it back to **Response** (the AI's answer) each time.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

</Accordion>

### Sharing with your partner

<Accordion type="single" collapsible>

<AccordionItem value="folder-not-visible">
<AccordionTrigger>Your partner can't see the shared folder</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Check they've accepted the folder invite, and that they're signed in to iCloud with Notes turned on. Folder sharing is Apple-to-Apple only, so if they're on Android, use the WhatsApp/message route instead.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="no-to-field">
<AccordionTrigger>Tapping Recipients on the Send Message action doesn't show a "To" field</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Delete the **Send Message** action and add it again; it usually comes back correctly. Also, make sure you tap the "Recipients" variable briefly, not tap and hold.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="asterisks">
<AccordionTrigger>The category headings show asterisks instead of bold in the message</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Single-asterisk bold only renders in **WhatsApp and Telegram**. In email and some apps the `*Logistics*` shows as plain characters. If you don't want the asterisks, remove them from the headings in the **Text** action: `Logistics`.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="empty-heading">
<AccordionTrigger>A category heading appears with nothing under it</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Whatever you dictated didn't include items for that category, so it remained empty. It's harmless, just a bare heading.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

</Accordion>

### The Baby Log

<Accordion type="single" collapsible>

<AccordionItem value="nothing-appends">
<AccordionTrigger>Nothing appends to the Baby Log note</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Check the note exists (first line **Baby Log**), that your **Append to Note** action in the shortcut has the **Baby Log** note picked in its note field, and that the text is the **Response** from Use Model.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="no-date">
<AccordionTrigger>The lines have no date or time</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

The date and time come from two actions that sit before **Use Model**: **Current Date**, then **Format Date** (tap its blue arrow and set both **Date Format** and **Time Format** to **Short**). If those two actions are missing, add them. If they're there, the **Formatted Date** chip just isn't in your prompt: place it right after "the date and time now is:".

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="no-chatgpt">
<AccordionTrigger>ChatGPT isn't available in the model picker</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Turn on the extension on your phone: **Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri → ChatGPT (under Extensions) → Set Up → Next → Enable ChatGPT**.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="babylog-cut-off">
<AccordionTrigger>The shortcut stops before you finish talking</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

The Baby Log uses in the **Dictate Text** action **Stop Listening → After Pause**, which ends right when you pause, that suits a short log line. If you want longer, switch that action to **On Tap** so you tap the stop button when you're done. Keep the screen awake while you talk, if it dims mid-dictation, it won't save your words.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

</Accordion>

### Hands-free triggers

<Accordion type="single" collapsible>

<AccordionItem value="siri-cuts-off">
<AccordionTrigger>"Hey Siri, Brain Dump" cuts you off when you pause to think</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Siri ends the dictation the moment you take a breath. For the pause-heavy Brain Dump, start it by tapping the Home Screen icon, Back Tap, or the Action Button instead. Hey Siri suits the short Baby Log.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="siri-nothing">
<AccordionTrigger>"Hey Siri" does nothing</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Siri needs to be enabled. On your phone go to: **Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri**, turn on **Talk to Siri** (or **Listen for "Hey Siri"**).

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

<AccordionItem value="back-tap">
<AccordionTrigger>Back Tap doesn't fire the shortcut</AccordionTrigger>
<AccordionContent>

Tap the back of the phone **firmly**, and make sure the screen is on (not black) first. It works even from the lock screen once set in **Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap**.

</AccordionContent>
</AccordionItem>

</Accordion>
