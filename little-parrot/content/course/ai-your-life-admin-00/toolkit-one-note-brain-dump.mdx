## Brain Dump into a single list

A stripped-back version of the Brain Dump for when you don't want three categories, just one running checklist. You talk, and everything you said lands as tidy, tickable lines in a single note. It's the same idea as the full build, with the sorting taken out, so it's quicker to make.

### 1. Make one note

Open **Notes** and make a note with **Brain Dump** as its first line. That's the note everything will land in.

### 2. Add Dictate Text

Open **Shortcuts**, tap **+**, and add **Dictate Text**. Tap the blue arrow, set your **Language**, and set **Stop Listening** to **On Tap** so it keeps listening through your pauses.

![Adding the Dictate Text action](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-dictate-text.webp)

### 3. Add Use Model

Add **Use Model**, tap the model and choose **Cloud (Private Cloud Compute)**, and set the **Output** to **Text** (you want plain lines back, not sorted categories). Paste this prompt, then add the **Dictated Text** chip at the very end:

```
Turn what I say into a tidy checklist, one item per line. Keep the real details I gave and drop the filler. Don't add anything I didn't say.

Here is what I said:
```

### 4. Add Append Checklist Item

Add **Append Checklist Item**. Put the **Response** from Use Model in as the item (each line becomes its own tickable line), and tap the **Note** field to pick your **Brain Dump** note.

### 5. Skip the accidental taps

Wrap the lot so an empty tap does nothing. Add an **If**, delete its **Otherwise**, set it to **Dictated Text** → **has any value**, then drag the **If** up under **Dictate Text** and the **End If** to the very bottom, so Use Model and Append sit inside it.

### 6. Name it and run it

Rename it **Brain Dump** and tap the **tick** (✓) on the keyboard. Add it to your Home Screen, tap it, talk, and tap the stop button when you're done. Everything you said turns up as one tidy checklist.

**Want to sort into Logistics, Food & Household and Misc instead?** That's the full build, which uses a Dictionary output and one block per category. It's the version taught in the course.
