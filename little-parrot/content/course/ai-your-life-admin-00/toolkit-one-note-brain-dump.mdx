## Brain Dump into a Single List

Brain Dump into a Single List is an iPhone Shortcut that turns your mental load into one tidy checklist. You tap it, talk through everything on your mind, and your phone uses Apple's built-in AI to turn it into tickable lines in a single note. It's the simple cousin of the full Brain Dump, which sorts what you say into three separate category notes. This one keeps everything in one list, so it's quicker to make.

*You'll need an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence: an **iPhone 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max, any iPhone 16 or newer, or an iPhone Air**. Everything else is built in, with nothing to buy or download.*

### 1. Make one note

Open the **Notes** app and make a note with **Brain Dump** as its first line. That's the note everything will land in.

### 2. Add Dictate Text

Open the **Shortcuts** app, tap **+**, and add the **Dictate Text** action. Tap the blue arrow, set your **Language**, and set **Stop Listening** to **On Tap** so it keeps listening through your pauses.

![Adding the Dictate Text action](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-dictate-text.webp)

### 3. Add Use Model

Add **Use Model**, tap the model and choose **Cloud (Private Cloud Compute)**, and set the **Output** to **Text** (you want plain lines back, not sorted categories). Paste this prompt, then add the **Dictated Text** chip at the very end:

```
Turn what I say into a tidy checklist, one item per line. Keep the real details I gave and drop the filler. Don't add anything I didn't say.

Here is what I said:
```

### 4. Add Append Checklist Item

Add **Append Checklist Item**. Put the **Response** from Use Model in as the item (each line becomes its own tickable line), and tap the **Note** field to pick your **Brain Dump** note.

### 5. Skip the accidental taps

Wrap the actions so an empty tap doesn't create a made-up list. Add an **If**, delete its **Otherwise** section, set it to **Dictated Text** → **has any value**, then drag the **If** up under **Dictate Text** and the **End If** to the very bottom, so Use Model and Append Checklist Item sit inside it.

### 6. Name it

Rename it **Brain Dump** and tap the **tick** (✓) on the keyboard.

### 7. Add it to your Home Screen and run it

So you can tap it like an app, add it to your Home Screen:

1. While editing the shortcut, tap the **Share** icon at the **bottom** of the screen.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Give it a **colour** and a **symbol** so it's easy to spot, then tap **Add**.

Now tap the **Brain Dump** icon on your Home Screen to run it, talk through everything on your mind, then tap the stop button (⏹️) when you're done. Everything you said turns up as one tidy checklist.

![Running your shortcut from the Home Screen](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/run-the-shortcut.webp)
