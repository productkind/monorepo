## Build the Baby Log Shortcut

The Baby Log is an iPhone Shortcut that turns a few spoken words into a timestamped record of your baby's day. You tap it, say the feed, the nappy, the nap, and your phone uses AI to save it as neat, categorised lines in one running note you can scroll back through or show your doctor. This guide has every step, in order, so you can build it from scratch or rebuild it later.

You'll need an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence: an **iPhone 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max, any iPhone 16 or newer, or an iPhone Air**. Everything else is built in, with nothing to buy or download.

### 1. Make the note first

Open **Notes** and make one note with **Baby Log** as its first line. It only needs to exist; your shortcut will add to it.

![Making the Baby Log note](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/make-baby-log-note.webp)

### 2. Turn on ChatGPT

The Baby Log uses **ChatGPT**, because it lays out neat, categorised lines more consistently than Apple's own model. It's built into your phone, with nothing to download and no account needed.

1. Go to **Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri → Extensions → ChatGPT**.
2. Tap **Set Up**, tap **Next**, then tap **Enable ChatGPT**.

(If you'd like your dictations to show up among your ChatGPT chats, choose **Use ChatGPT with an Account** and sign in instead. Either works.)

![Turning on the ChatGPT extension](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/turn-on-chatgpt.webp)

### 3. Copy the logging prompt

This tells the AI to split what you say into activities and stamp the session with the time. Copy it now:

```
You are a baby care log parser. Turn the voice entry below into clean, scannable log lines.

The date and time now is:

Rules:
- Begin with the date and time above on its own line, in bold, with a | between the date and the time, like: **07/08/2026 | 13:36**
- Under it, add one line per activity I mention, formatted as: - **Category**: details.
- Use whichever of these categories fit: Feed, Nappy, Nap, Mood, Weight, Milestone. Use Note if none fit.
- Keep the real details I gave and drop the filler. Don't invent anything.
- If I didn't mention anything worth logging, reply with nothing.
- Reply with just the heading and the lines, nothing else.

Here is what I said:
```

### 4. Talk, stamp the time, then sort

Open **Shortcuts**, tap **+**, and add these blocks in order:

1. **Dictate Text.** Tap the blue arrow, set your **Language**, and set **Stop Listening** to **After Pause**, since a log entry is short and can end on its own.
2. **Current Date**, then **Format Date** right after it. Tap the blue arrow on Format Date and set both **Date Format** and **Time Format** to **Short**, so you get today's date and the time in an easy-to-read format.
3. **Use Model.** Tap the model and choose **ChatGPT** (leave the Output on **Automatic**). Paste your logging prompt, then place the **Formatted Date** chip right after "the date and time now is:", and add the **Dictated Text** chip at the very end.

![Building the dictate, date and model blocks](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-babylog-model.webp)

### 5. Save each session to your note

Add **Append to Note**. Tap the note field and pick your **Baby Log** note, and put the **Response** from Use Model in as the text. It adds to the bottom, so sessions stack up newest last.

![Adding the Append to Note action](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-babylog-append.webp)

### 6. Name it and run it

Rename it **Baby Log** and tap the **tick** (✓) on the keyboard. Run it whenever something happens, say the feed and how much, a nappy, a nap, and each tap adds a timestamped session like:

```
**07/08/2026 | 13:36**
- **Feed**: 200 mL
- **Mood**: a bit fussy after eating
- **Nap**: went down to sleep
```

Prefer to keep everything on your phone and share nothing with another company? Swap **ChatGPT** for **Cloud (Private Cloud Compute)** in the Use Model block. It's fully private, though it lays the lines out less tidily.
