## Build the Brain Dump Shortcut

The Brain Dump is an iPhone Shortcut that turns your mental load into organised lists. You tap it, talk through everything on your mind, and your phone uses Apple's built-in AI to **sort what you said into checklist notes**: *Logistics, Food & Household, and Misc*. You can **share those notes** with your partner, so the mental load isn't yours alone to carry. This guide has every step, in order, so you can build it from scratch or rebuild it later.

*You'll need an iPhone that runs Apple Intelligence: an **iPhone 15 Pro or 15 Pro Max, any iPhone 16 or newer, or an iPhone Air**. Everything else is built in, with nothing to buy or download.*

### 1. Turn on Apple Intelligence

Open the **Settings** app, go to **Apple Intelligence & Siri**, and turn on **Apple Intelligence**. It's free, with no account, and it's what does the sorting. (It needs to be available in your country and language.)

![Turning on Apple Intelligence in Settings](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/turn-on-apple-intelligence.webp)

### 2. Make one folder and three notes

Open the **Notes** app and make a folder called **Mental Load**. Inside it, make one note per category, with each note's first line exactly: **Logistics**, **Food & Household**, and **Misc**. You'll pick these notes as you build, so keep the names consistent.

![The Mental Load folder with three category notes](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/make-folder-and-notes.webp)

### 3. Copy the sorting prompt

This is the instruction you'll give the AI. Copy it now; you'll paste it in later.

```
Sort what I say into a dictionary with exactly these keys: Logistics, Food & Household, Misc.

- Food & Household: anything to buy, get, or restock, including food, drink, and household supplies.
- Logistics: appointments, bookings, and calls to make (not shopping).
- Misc: anything that doesn't fit the other two.

Each value is a list, one item per entry. Trim filler words, but keep the details that make each item make sense on its own later: what it is, why, and any time or day. Don't add anything I didn't say.

Here is what I said:
```

You can reword the category definitions or add your own categories later, as long as your notes use the same names.

### 4. Dictate Text

Open the **Shortcuts** app, tap **+** to start a new shortcut, and add the **Dictate Text** action. Tap the blue arrow to open its settings, set your **Language**, and set **Stop Listening** to **On Tap** so it keeps listening through your pauses until you tap the stop button (⏹️).

![Adding the Dictate Text action](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-dictate-text.webp)

### 5. Use Model

Add the **Use Model** action. Tap the model and choose **Cloud (Private Cloud Compute)**. Tap the blue arrow and set the **Output** to **Dictionary**, so the answer comes back as your labelled categories.

![Setting Use Model to Cloud and Output to Dictionary](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-use-model.webp)

### 6. Paste the prompt

In the **Use Model** prompt box, delete the **Dictated Text** chip that's already there, paste your sorting prompt, then tap to add the **Dictated Text** chip back at the very end, after "Here is what I said:".

![Pasting the prompt with the Dictated Text chip at the end](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-paste-prompt.webp)

### 7. Route the first category (Logistics)

Add these four actions in order, all for **Logistics**:

1. **Get Dictionary Value**: type **Logistics** as the **Key**, and set the **Dictionary** field to the **Response**.
2. **Combine Text**: it takes the **Dictionary Value** above; set it to combine with **New Lines**, so each item sits on its own line.
3. **If**: set its condition to **Combined Text**, then tap **is** and change it to **has any value**. Delete the **Otherwise** line. This skips a category when you said nothing for it.
4. **Append Checklist Item** (drag it in between the **If** and **End If**): put your **Combined Text** in as the **Name**, and tap the **Note** field to pick your **Logistics** note directly. Each line becomes its own tickable item.

![The five-action Logistics block](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-route-category.webp)

### 8. Test it before you copy it

Tap the **▶️ play button** at the bottom, say one or two Logistics things (like *"call the dentist, book a yoga class"*), then tap the stop button. Open your **Logistics** note; the items should be there as checklist lines. If the note stayed empty, check the **Append Checklist Item** points at your **Logistics** note and that **Get Dictionary Value** reads from **Response**.

![Testing the first block](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-test-first-block.webp)

### 9. Do the same for Food & Household, then Misc

Build the same block again for each remaining category. Only the **category name** changes.

**Food & Household:**
1. **Get Dictionary Value**: type **Food & Household** as the **Key**, and set the **Dictionary** field to the **Response**.
2. **Combine Text**: the **Dictionary Value** above, combined with **New Lines**.
3. **If**: **Combined Text** → **has any value**, then delete the **Otherwise** line.
4. **Append Checklist Item** (between the **If** and **End If**): **Combined Text** as the **Name**, and pick your **Food & Household** note.

**Misc:** exactly the same, with **Misc** as the **Key** and your **Misc** note. You now have three blocks, one per category.

![Repeating the block for the other categories](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-repeat-categories.webp)

### 10. Skip the accidental taps

Wrap everything after **Dictate Text** in one guard so an empty tap does nothing (without it, the AI invents a random list to fill the categories):

1. Add an **If**, delete its **Otherwise** line, and set it to **Dictated Text** → **has any value**.
2. Drag the **If** up so it sits right under **Dictate Text**, and drag the **End If** to the very bottom, so every other action is inside it.

*If the End If won't stay at the bottom* and springs back above your last block, add any action at the very end (a **Combine Text** will do), drag the **End If** just above it, then delete that extra action.

![Wrapping the shortcut in the outer If guard](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-if-guard.webp)

### 11. Name it

Tap the name at the top of the screen, select **Rename**, type **Brain Dump**, and tap the **tick** (✓) on the keyboard.

![Naming the shortcut](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/build-finish.webp)

### 12. Add it to your Home Screen and run it

So you can tap it like an app, add it to your Home Screen:

1. While editing the shortcut, tap the **Share** icon at the **bottom** of the screen.
2. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Give it a **colour** and a **symbol** so it's easy to spot, then tap **Add**.

Now tap the **Brain Dump** icon on your Home Screen to run it, talk about everything on your mind in any order, then tap the stop button (⏹️) when you're done. Keep the screen on while you talk, if it dims mid-dictation, it won't save your dictation.

![Running your shortcut from the Home Screen](/courses/ai-your-life-admin-00/run-the-shortcut.webp)

That's the whole build. If a step misbehaves, the Troubleshooting Your Shortcuts toolkit item covers the common issues.

**Want your partner to get these lists on a phone that isn't an iPhone?** The [Send Your List to Your Partner](https://littleparrot.app/nest/toolkit/69103a0a-1e68-4db2-ad24-4ff619f10d52) guide adds an automatic message to your shortcut.
